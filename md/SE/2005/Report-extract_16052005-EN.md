Extract from the investigation report RJ 2009:10 on a fire on 
undergrouond train occurred on 16/05/2005 
Summary  
Shortly after 08.00 on 16 May 2005 a fire broke out in the underframe of an underground 
wagon as the train containing the wagon was standing in Rinkeby underground station. The 
fire was noticed by a passenger, who observed significant noise and light phenomena. The 
passenger contacted the driver, who observed that there was smoke under the wagon. The 
underground station was evacuated, and the emergency services were called, searched for 
any remaining passengers and extinguished the fire. No-one was seriously injured.  
Comprehensive technical investigations were carried out to establish the cause of the fire. The 
investigations were, however, made considerably more difficult by the major damage to the 
vehicle.  
It was considered nonetheless that the fire had in all probability started as a result of arcing 
occurring in the underframe of the wagon. The reason for the arcing occurring was thought to 
be an electric short-cut.  
Statens haverikommission [the Swedish Accident Investigation Board] (SHK) also examined 
the evacuation of the station and the organisation and safety management of the Stockholm 
Metro.  
The report further finds that, where several operators are working on the underground system,  
there is a need for a common safety management system for the operators working on the 
underground system. 
Causes  
The direct cause of the accident was an electric short-cut in the vehicle combined with the fact 
that the rail power supply protection functions were turned off.  
The underlying causes were that isolating switch 3335-33411 was in the wrong position 
(closed) in connection with preparatory work for the SNOK system and was not affected by the 
emergency cut-out activated by the controller.  
The organisational causes were that there was no system ensuring that the connection 
position was correct in terms of the planned position. There was no integrated safety 
management system for the firms undertakings on the underground, which means that 
common risks were not dealt with. 
Recommendations  
It is recommended that Transportstyrelsen [the Swedish Transport Agency] review the need for 
clearer regulation of the requirement for safety management systems, approval of technical 
systems and handling of common risks for operators where there are several different active 
operators, for example on the underground system (RJ 2009:10 R1).

It is recommended that the Swedish Transport Agency and Elsäkerhetsverket [the Swedish 
National Electrical Safety Board] ensure that traffic operators examine the design of the 
C14 vehicle to ensure the vehicle’s function throughout its remaining lifespan (RJ 2009:10 R2),  
It is recommended that Myndigheten för samhällsskydd och beredskap [the Swedish Civil 
Contingencies Agency] (MSB):  
 
ensures the development of a tactical action plan to facilitate fire extinguishing and 
rescue/evacuation of more than a small number of injured persons from platform level in an 
underground station (RJ 2009:10 R3); 
 
ensures that guidelines are drawn up in conjunction with Arbetsmiljöverket [the 
Swedish Work Environment Authority] and Boverket [the National Board of Housing, Building 
and Planning] to ensure the necessary evacuation facilities in complex public environments 
located underground, for example the existing underground railway, by issuing clear 
instructions concerning inter alia (RJ 2009:10 R4): - that evacuation routes, e.g. escalator 
shafts, are protected against filling with smoke, - evacuation alarms, - exit signs and 
emergency lighting, - that emergency evacuation routes are accessible; 
 
ensures that supervisory work is prioritised so as to demonstrate clearly how the 
municipality’s rescue service is organised and planned so that rescue operations can begin 
within an acceptable time and be carried out effectively. Supervision should also include 
following up how the municipality cooperates with other interested parties regarding planning, 
training, operational exercises, tactical design of operations and sounding alarms so as to be 
able to deal with large-scale and complex operational situations such as accidents on the 
underground (RJ 2009:10 R5).
