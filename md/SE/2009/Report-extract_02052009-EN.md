Extract from the investigation report RJ 2011:01 on an incident 
occurred on 02/05/2009 
Summary 
On Saturday, 2 May 2009, an incident occurred involving wagons rolling uncontrolled on the 
Östavall - Alby line.  
The wagons had earlier that day been transported from Östavall to the timber terminal in Töva, 
and then run back again to the timber terminal in Östavall to be loaded with timber.  
When the train came to Östavall, the driver had to move the locomotive to the other end of the 
trainset in order to back to the wagons to the timber terminal.  
After the driver had backed in to the timber terminal in Östavall, he decoupled the wagons and 
parked them in the terminal. He then drove the locomotive to Ånge at 14:35 as train 85090.  
A private individual who was in the vicinity of the railway in Östavall noticed that wagons were 
rolling and at 15:27 contacted the remote dispatcher in Ånge to give notice that there were 
wagons rolling in Östavall.  
Just prior to the call, the remote dispatcher had noticed that there was a shorted track circuit in 
Östavall and had begun to investigate what might have happened.  
The wagons rolled about 4 km on the up track before they stopped. The incident caused no 
injuries, but if a train had been on the up track towards Östavall then a collision could have 
occurred. 
Recommendations 
It is recommended that the Transport Agency:  
 
explore the possibility of developing standards for how protection should be arranged for 
parking tracks to prevent vehicles from rolling out onto/near to the connecting main line, 
even when there is not a reserved train set on the main line (see sections 2.3.2 and 3.3 B2) 
(RJ 2011:01 R1).  
 
in connection with inspections, investigate whether operators' safety management systems 
are sufficient to capture behaviours that can endanger traffic safety (see sections 2.2.3, 3.2.1 
A1, 3.2.2 H1 and 3.3 B1) (RJ 2011:01 R2).
