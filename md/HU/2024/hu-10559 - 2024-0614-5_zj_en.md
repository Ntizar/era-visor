### Basic principles of the safety investigation 
The purpose of the safety investigation fulfilled by Transportation Safety Bureau (TSB) as 
National Investigation Body of Hungary is to reveal the causes and circumstances of serious 
railway accidents, railway accidents and railway incidents and propose recommendations in 
order to prevent similar incidents. The safety investigation is not intended to examine and 
determine fault, blame or liability in any form. 
The findings of the safety investigation are based on an assessment of the evidence available 
and obtained by TSB in the course of the investigation, taking into account the principles of a 
fair and impartial procedure. In the Final Report, the persons involved in the occurrence shall 
be referred to by the positions and duties they had at the time of the occurrence. 
The Final Report shall not have binding force and no appeal proceedings may be initiated 
against it. 
This safety investigation has been carried out by TSB pursuant to relevant provisions of 
- 
Act CLXXXIV of 2005 on the safety investigation of aviation, railway and marine 
accidents and incidents; 
- 
Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/572 of 24 April 2020 on the reporting 
structure to be followed for railway accident and incident investigation reports; 
- 
in the absence of other related regulation of the Act CLXXXIV of 2005, the TSB 
conducts the investigation in accordance with Act CL of 2016 on General Public 
Administration Procedures. 
Act CLXXXIV of 2005 is to serve compliance with Directive (EU) 2016/798 of the European 
Parliament and of the Council of 11 May 2016 on railway safety. 
The competence of the TSB is based on Government Regulation № 230/2016. (VII.29.) on the 
assignment of a transportation safety body and on the dissolution of Transportation Safety 
Bureau with legal succession. 
The safety investigation is independent of other investigations, administrative infringement or 
criminal proceedings, as well as proceedings initiated by employers in connection with the 
accident or incident.

## Página 3

### 1. 
### SUMMARY 
At 19:18 on 7 June 2024, at Kőbánya felső station, train No. 553 passed the entrance 
signal marked "Y" at danger, without permission, and entered track VI of the station. The 
switch was not burst open and there was no train endangerment during the occurrence. 
As a result of the incident, the rest of the route of train No. 553 was cancelled, and three 
other trains were cancelled for their entire routes, but there were no train delays. 
Prior to the incident, between the Rákos and Kőbánya felső stations, the locomotive of 
train No. IC656-1 became inoperable on the correct left track of the so-called lower 
tracks, so train No. 553 was run on the incorrect right track. The safety equipment only 
allows trains to run on the incorrect track of the lower tracks in the station-to-station 
mode, with danger signals. 
The reason for the incident was that the locomotive driver of train No. 553 misinterpreted 
the written instructions received at Rákos station and therefore passed the entry signal 
at danger at Kőbánya felső station. 
A factor contributing to the incident was that, although there is an alternative route 
between Rákos and Kőbánya felső stations (the so-called upper tracks) for travelling 
under the supervision of the safety equipment and with signals allowing trains to proceed 
at the time of the incident, but running trains on the upper tracks instead of the lower 
tracks means extra paper work for the traffic management staff, which encourages them 
to go for the less safe option. 
In view of the instruction amendment implemented at the time of the investigation, the 
TSB does not consider it necessary to issue a safety recommendation.

### 5. 
### CONCLUSIONS 
### 5.1 
### Summary 
## 5.1.1 
### Direct causes 
Acts, mistakes, events or conditions or a combination thereof the elimination or 
avoiding of which could probably have prevented the accident or incident: 
a) The locomotive driver of train No. 553 passed the entry signal (marked "Y") 
at danger at Kőbánya felső station without permission because 
− he misinterpreted the written instructions received at Rákos station; 
− he did not have sufficient experience and expertise in the railway 
system to handle the complex traffic situation appropriately. 
## 5.1.2 
### Indirect causes 
Acts, mistakes, events or conditions which influenced the occurrence by increasing 
its probability, accelerating the effects or the severity of the consequences, but the 
elimination of which would not have prevented the occurrence: 
a) Between Rákos and Kőbánya felső stations, it is not possible to travel on the 
wrong track on the "lower tracks" with signals allowing trains to proceed. 
b) After recognising the dangerous situation, the traffic controller did not begin 
his telephone conversation with the locomotive driver by instructing him to 
stop. 
## 5.1.3 
### Systemic factors 
Causal or contributing factors of organisational, management, social or regulatory 
nature which are likely to have an effect on similar or related occurrences, 
particularly including regulatory framework conditions, the design and use of the 
safety management systems, the skills of the personnel, the procedures and 
maintenance: 
a) Between the Rákos and Kőbánya felső stations, running trains on the upper 
track instead of the lower track meant additional administrative work, which 
encouraged traffic personnel to use a solution that offered a lower level of 
safety; 
b) Changes to this procedure were delayed due to the slow implementation of 
IT developments. 
c) The examination system based on knowledge of instructions is not capable 
of revealing knowledge, understanding and correct application of the rules. 
### 5.2 
### Actions taken 
The railway companies did not report any measures taken in connection with the 
incident to improve the overall safety of rail transport. 
### 5.3 
### Additional notes 
The following factors cannot be linked to the incident, but they do increase risk: 
a) the locomotive driver may have acquired knowledge of line 200 without ever 
having travelled on that section of the line; 
b) the railway company’s route book contains incorrect data. 

## Página 5

### 5.4 
### Proven procedures, good practices 
It helped to reduce the cosequences of the occurence and avoid a more serious 
outcome that 
a) At Kőbánya felső station, train No. 553, which entered without permission, 
was unable to enter the track, owing to the closed protective switches of train 
No. IC656-1, which was entering from the correct track. 
### 5.5 
### Lessons learnt 
The incident highlights that even a minor malfunction can lead to complex traffic 
situations, and resolving situations that differ from the norm can be challenging for 
employees who are not adequately prepared. However, a well-functioning 
organisation can be expected to encourage staff to use the safer option, if available. 
### 6. 
### SAFETY RECOMMENDATION 
Such cases can be avoided by complying with the rules (which ones?) and by the 
staff exercising due care, therefore the IC does not consider it necessary to issue a 
safety recommendation.
