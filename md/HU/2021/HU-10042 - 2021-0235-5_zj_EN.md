### Basic principles of the safety investigation 
The purpose of the safety investigation fulfilled by Transportation Safety Bureau (TSB) as 
National Investigation Body of Hungary is to reveal the causes and circumstances of serious 
railway accidents, railway accidents and railway incidents and propose recommendations in 
order to prevent similar incidents. The safety investigation is not intended to examine and 
determine fault, blame or liability in any form. 
The findings of the safety investigation are based on an assessment of the evidence 
available and obtained by TSB in the course of the investigation, taking into account the 
principles of a fair and impartial procedure. In the Final Report, the persons involved in the 
occurrence shall be referred to by the positions and duties they had at the time of the 
occurrence. 
The Final Report shall not have binding force and no appeal proceedings may be initiated 
against it. 
This safety investigation has been carried out by TSB pursuant to relevant provisions of 
 
Act CLXXXIV of 2005 on the safety investigation of aviation, railway and marine 
accidents and incidents; 
 
Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/572 of 24 April 2020 on the 
reporting structure to be followed for railway accident and incident investigation 
reports; 
 
in the absence of other related regulation of the Act CLXXXIV of 2005, the TSB 
conducts the investigation in accordance with Act CL of 2016 on General Public 
Administration Procedures. 
Act CLXXXIV of 2005 is to serve compliance with Directive (EU) 2016/798 of the European 
Parliament and of the Council of 11 May 2016 on railway safety. 
The competence of the TSB is based on Government Regulation № 230/2016. (VII.29.) on 
the assignment of a transportation safety body and on the dissolution of Transportation 
Safety Bureau with legal succession. 
The safety investigation is independent of other investigations, administrative infringement or 
criminal proceedings, as well as proceedings initiated by employers in connection with the 
accident or incident.

## Página 3

### 1. 
### SUMMARY 
On 10 March 2021, at 12:14, when arriving at Rákos station from the direction of 
Rákoshegy, the freight train № 44290-1 passed the entry signal N at danger 
without authorisation. The train stopped in the railway section № 15+60, past the 
shunting limit signal of the first trailing point. 
According to the IC, the locomotive driver did not respond to the third vigilance 
alert following the pre-indication of the block signal for the entry signal at danger, 
therefore the vigilance warning and train control device initiated braking, but it was 
ineffective due to technical malfunction. Such irregular operation distracted the 
locomotive driver’s attention. 
The railway undertaking had been aware of the uncertain operation of the vigilance 
warning and train control device prior to the occurrence, but they failed to take 
action to clearly identify and eliminate the error. 
In addition, the railway undertaking had failed to meet the requirement (included in 
its safety management system) relating to the inspection of records from on-board 
data recorders, which is important in that such records clearly showed the signs of 
errors made by the vigilance warning and train control device. 
TSB issues a safety recommendation regarding the review of the railway 
undertaking’s safety management system as well as its operation in practice.

### 5. 
### CONCLUSIONS 
### 5.1 
### Summary 
## 5.1.1 
### Direct causes 
Acts, mistakes, events or conditions or a combination thereof the elimination or 
avoiding of which could probably have prevented the accident or incident: 
a) the locomotive driver did not prepare for stopping in front of the 
signal at danger; 
b) the vigilance warning and train control device did not brake the train 
after the locomotive driver failed to respond to the vigilance alert; 
c) despite being aware of the defect, the railway undertaking failed to 
arrange for identification and elimination of the error in the 
locomotive. 
## 5.1.2 
### Indirect causes 
Acts, mistakes, events or conditions which influenced the occurrence by increasing 
its probability, accelerating the effects or the severity of the consequences, but the 
elimination of which would not have prevented the occurrence: 
a) the vigilance warning and train control device of the locomotive had 
a malfunction which made its operation unreliable and had the 
potential to distract the locomotive driver’s attention. 
## 5.1.3 
### Systemic factors 
Causal or contributing factors of organisational, management, social or regulatory 
nature which are likely to have an effect on similar or related occurrences, 
particularly including regulatory framework conditions, the design and use of the 
safety management systems, the skills of the personnel, the procedures and 
maintenance: 
a) the railway undertaking has not met the requirement relating to the 
inspection of recordings from on-board data recorders as included 
in its safety management system; 
b) the safety management system does not specify a register which 
would serve traceability of malfunctions of locomotives, and the 
railway undertaking keeps no such records. 
### 5.2 
### Actions taken 
Following the occurrence, the pneumatic points and electrical disabling points of 
the vigilance warning and train control device were equipped with numbered 
plastic locks, and the railway undertaking plans to do the same with all its 
locomotives. 
The railway undertaking sent the locomotive driver for a psychological evaluation 
which found no cause that would exclude his fitness for work. 
### 5.3 
### Additional notes 
The IC identified no factors which do not relate to the occurrence but increase risk. 

## Página 5

### 5.4 
### Proven procedures, good practices 
It served to mitigate the consequences of the case and to prevent more serious 
outcomes that: 
a) when realising the emergency situation, the traffic manager closed 
the affected barrier № SR1. 
### 5.5 
### Lessons learnt 
 
By finding eliminating the actual defect after detecting signs of erroneous 
operation, unsafe locomotives could be prevented from participating in 
traffic for long periods of time. 
 
In a locomotive driver’s work, approaching a signal at danger is a phase of 
high risk where distraction of attention is especially hazardous. However, 
one can deliberately prepare to manage and exclude distraction factors. 
 
The availability of a manual which contains a description of the safety 
management system does not in itself provide the safety of railway 
transport. To achieve safety, it should actually describe the processes in a 
practicable manner, and managers, supervisors and all the staff involved 
should be aware of it, and they should intend to observe it and to have it 
observed.

### 6.1 
### BA2020-0304-5-01 
The IC found during the investigation that the safety management system (and 
practice) of the railway undertaking did not provide accurate monitoring and 
elimination of vehicle defects; in addition, the railway undertaking does not perform 
the required inspection of recordings from the on-board data recorders on a routine 
basis.
