### Basic principles of the safety investigation 
The purpose of the safety investigation fulfilled by Transportation Safety Bureau (TSB) as 
National Investigation Body of Hungary is to reveal the causes and circumstances of serious 
railway accidents, railway accidents and railway incidents and propose recommendations in 
order to prevent similar incidents. The safety investigation is not intended to examine and 
determine fault, blame or liability in any form. 
The findings of the safety investigation are based on an assessment of the evidence 
available and obtained by TSB in the course of the investigation, taking into account the 
principles of a fair and impartial procedure. In the Final Report, the persons involved in the 
occurrence shall be referred to by the positions and duties they had at the time of the 
occurrence. 
The Final Report shall not have binding force and no appeal proceedings may be initiated 
against it. 
This safety investigation has been carried out by TSB pursuant to relevant provisions of 
- 
Act CLXXXIV of 2005 on the safety investigation of aviation, railway and marine 
accidents and incidents; 
- 
Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/572 of 24 April 2020 on the 
reporting structure to be followed for railway accident and incident investigation 
reports; 
- 
in the absence of other related regulation of the Act CLXXXIV of 2005, the TSB 
conducts the investigation in accordance with Act CL of 2016 on General Public 
Administration Procedures. 
Act CLXXXIV of 2005 is to serve compliance with Directive (EU) 2016/798 of the European 
Parliament and of the Council of 11 May 2016 on railway safety. 
The competence of the TSB is based on Government Regulation № 230/2016. (VII.29.) on 
the assignment of a transportation safety body and on the dissolution of Transportation 
Safety Bureau with legal succession. 
The safety investigation is independent of other investigations, administrative infringement or 
criminal proceedings, as well as proceedings initiated by employers in connection with the 
accident or incident.

## Página 3

### 1. 
### SUMMARY 
At 00:19 a.m. on 10 July 2022, on a track equipped for block signal based mode, 
between Biatorbágy and Herceghalom stations, the freight train № 48400 caught 
up and collided with the freight train № 45286 which was stationary in the third 
block section. 
The incident resulted in no personal injuries, but 7 wagons of the train at the front 
derailed, the railway track was damaged, and the wreckage blocked both tracks. 
The passenger train № 4961, approaching from the opposite direction, was held 
back at Herceghalom station. The railway line was closed to traffic for 42 hours. 
The IC found that the driver of the freight train № 48400, after several actions that 
were objectionable from the point of view of driving technique, applied the brake 
late in front of the block signal 338a, and passed it at 23 km/h, so the train control 
equipment stopped the train by applying the emergency brake. The driver then 
deactivated the train control device, had a discussion with the traffic officer at 
Herceghalom station and then - in the occupied block section - continued on his 
way, accelerating to 43-45 km/h. When he saw the end of the freight train 45268 in 
front of him, he could not stop and crashed into it. 
The IC attributed the cause of the occurrence to a human factor related to the 
locomotive driver, which could not be further investigated, and the key element of 
which was the disabling of the train control device (which was not allowed in this 
situation), and the choosing of an excessive speed. 
Since such occurrences can be avoided by observing the rules, driving carefully 
and using safety equipment correctly, the TSB does not issue safety 
recommendations.

### 5. 
### CONCLUSIONS 
### 5.1 
### Summary 
## 5.1.1 
### Direct causes 
Acts, mistakes, events or conditions or a combination thereof the elimination or 
avoiding of which could probably have prevented the accident or incident: 
a) The locomotive driver, while approaching the train in the occupied 
block section, was travelling at too high a speed to stop in time 
when he saw the end of the train in front of him, ... 
b) ...after entering the block section, he deactivated the train control 
equipment, which did not monitor or limit the applicable speed 
anymore. 
## 5.1.2 
### Indirect causes 
Acts, mistakes, events or conditions which influenced the occurrence by increasing 
its probability, accelerating the effects or the severity of the consequences, but the 
elimination of which would not have prevented the occurrence: 
a) The traffic on the railway network in the North Transdanubian region 
was more congested than usual due to simultaneous track closures 
at several points of the railway network, so trains were crowded up 
in the Biatorbágy-Herceghalom block section. 
## 5.1.3 
### Systemic factors 
Causal or contributing factors of organisational, management, social or regulatory 
nature which are likely to have an effect on similar or related occurrences, 
particularly including regulatory framework conditions, the design and use of the 
safety management systems, the skills of the personnel, the procedures and 
maintenance: no such factor has been identified by the IC. 
### 5.2 
### Actions taken 
The railway company reported the following actions related to the occurrence: 
 
An out-of-turn inspection of train running registers was carried out for the 
month of June 2022, specifically in relation to the use of the forward/reverse 
switch: some cases of its use contrary to instructions were found. Regular 
checks on the use of the switch were required for locomotive drivers appointed 
to traction. 
 
Training on the rules related to the occurrence was provided during the last 
quarterly training in 2022. 
 
On vehicles where the technical equipment allows it, the authorised/inspecting 
employee will be alerted when the forward/reverse switch is used. On those 
where this is not currently feasible, a technical solution has been found. An 
offer has been received, but the implementation of the software replacement 
has not yet been done due to the lack of an agreed business plan. 
 
There are plans to reinstate the travelling locomotive supervisor post previously 
used to monitor the activities of train drivers. The job has been created but, in 
the absence of an agreed business plan, no employment contract is yet 
possible.

### 5.3 
### Additional notes 
The IC identified no risk-increasing factors that could not be linked to the 
occurrence of the incident. 
### 5.4 
### Proven procedures, good practices 
No factors to reduce the consequences of the occurrence and avoid a more 
serious outcome have been identified by the IC. 
### 5.5 
### Lessons learnt 
There was a well-functioning safety device in place at the time of the incident to 
prevent such incidents; but disabling it, which is against the rules, will terminate 
such protection. When this is combined with inattentive and careless driver 
behaviour (the root cause of which the investigation has not been able to identify) it 
can easily lead to an accident. 
### 6. 
### SAFETY RECOMMENDATION 
Similar occurrences can be avoided by careful driving and proper operation of the 
safety equipment, and the IC therefore does not consider it justified to issue a 
safety recommendation.
