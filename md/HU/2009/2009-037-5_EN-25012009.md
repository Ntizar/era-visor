2009-037-5  
Railway accident  
On 25th January 2009, a passenger train collided with a car between Szil-Sopronnémeti and 
Csorna stations at an LC protected with warning lights.  
The driver of the car and one of its passengers died at the site of the accident, and the other 
passenger was taken into hospital with life-threatening injuries.  
Factual statements directly connected to the occurrence of the accident  
The accident occurred due to human factors related to the car driver.
