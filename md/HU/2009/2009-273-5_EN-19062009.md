2009-273-5  
On 19 June 2009 a regional passenger train collided with a trailer towed by a tractor at a 
normally operating LC between Tiszavasvári and Hajdúnánás stations.  
As a consequence of the accident, the motor coach of the train crashed and pushed the 
trailer aside which swept away a cyclist waiting at the LC.  
Factual statements directly connected to the occurrence of the accident  
The tractor drove into the LC regardless of the Stop signal of the warning lights.  
Other risk factors  
The visibility triangle at the LC was not ensured (from the road the tractor was driving on) 
from the prescribed distance, however, the signals of the warning lights were still visible.
