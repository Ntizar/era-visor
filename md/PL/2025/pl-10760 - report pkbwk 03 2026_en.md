# I. 
# SUMMARY 
### Type of occurrence: Incidents. 
Description: The occurrences took place on the mainline route Zarzeka–Puławy Azoty of railway line 
no. 7 Warszawa Wschodnia Osobowa – Dorohusk. 
The first occurrence took place on 16 November 2025 at 20:02 hrs as a result of theft, 
namely the cutting out of 60-metre sections of a single DJP contact wire on the tensioning 
cables at two locations: on track no. 1 from site 112-29 to site 112-27, and on track no. 2 
from site 112-30 to site 112-28. Cut out by unknown perpetrators, the sections were found 
in a drainage ditch alongside mainline tracks no. 2 and no. 1. 
Train MPE 83100/1 “GÓRSKI”, operating on the Świnoujście–Rzeszów Główny route 
for the carrier PKP Intercity S.A. and pulled by locomotive EU160-016, had been 
travelling on mainline track no. 1 to Puławy Azoty station and entered the area where the 
contact wire had been cut off at the auxiliary arm of overlap mast at site 112-29, which 
caused damage to the second pantograph in the direction of travel and severance of the 
overhead contact line on track no. 1 from site 112-31 to site 113-5 (i.e. from km 112.887 
to km 113.131). The train driver continued to drive the train coasting to stop halt at Puławy 
Azoty station at km 118.200. He reported the occurrence to the duty signaller at Puławy 
Azoty station saying that he had noticed a flash and loss of voltage on track no. 1 at km 
112.800. Subsequently, following the arrival of the network train, inspection of the 
locomotive and the securing of the damaged pantograph, the train continued its journey at 
23:23 hrs using the locomotive’s first pantograph. There were around 500 passengers on 
the train. Following the damage to parts of the overhead line and the pantograph on the 
locomotive, the entire Zarzeka–Puławy Azoty mainline route was inspected in order to 
assess the extent of the damage to the railway infrastructure. The inspection of the 
mainline route was carried out by a representative of IZ Lublin and the Railway Security 
Guard (SOK) with a police officer. During the inspection, at 23:53 hrs, a foreign steel 
object was found on track no. 1 at km 115.507. This was the second incident in which 
unknown perpetrators had placed a shaped metal plate over the head of the right-hand 
track rail and secured it from beneath the rail foot with a bolt. Next, a smartphone with a 
power bank were found at km 115.534 at the left-hand track rail, propped against the SHP 
guardrail. A steel bolt was found near the metal sheet attached to the track rail, lying on 
the outer trackside of the same track. None of these items were part of the railway 
infrastructure and have been recovered by the police for further investigation.

# II. THE INVESTIGATION AND ITS CONTEXT 
## 1. Decision to establish an investigation 
The Chairman of the State Commission on Railway Accident Investigation (hereinafter referred to as "PKBWK" 
or "the Commission") Tadeusz Ryś issued, pursuant to Article 28e(2) of the Act of 28 March 2003 on rail 
transport (consolidated text: Journal of Laws of 2025, item 1234), hereinafter referred to as "the Rail Transport 
Act", Decision No. PKBWK.590.6.2025 of 21 November 2025 on establishing an investigation into the 
circumstances of the occurrences that took place on 16 November 2025 on railway line no. 7 Warszawa 
Wschodnia – Dorohusk, mainline route Zarzeka – Puławy Azoty at 20:15 hrs at km 112.800 and at 23:53 hrs at 
km 115.500. 
The locations and times of the occurrences included in the Report are factual and confirmed by the investigation 
findings, and although they differ from the locations indicated in the notification and the decision, they relate to 
these occurrences.  In accordance with the provisions of Article 28e(4) of the Rail Transport Act, the occurrence 
was notified to the European Union Agency for Railways and registered in its database under no. PL-10760.  
## 2. Motivation for the decision to establish an investigation 
Taking into account the nature and circumstances of the occurrences, the Chairman of PKBWK decided that an 
investigation should be conducted by the Commission's Investigation Team pursuant to Article 28e(2) of the 
Rail Transport Act. The said occurrences, as railway incidents, relate to the system as a whole in accordance 
with Article 28e(3)(1) and (2) of the Railway Transport Act. Furthermore, the decision was supported by the 
nature of the incidents showing a high likelihood that the theft and installation of foreign objects on the track 
were linked to an act of sabotage. 
3. The scope and limits of the investigation including a justification thereof, as well 
as an explanation of any delay that is considered a risk or other impact to the 
conduct of the investigation or its conclusions 
The investigation of the causal, contributing and systemic factors was conducted under Article 28h(1) of the 
Rail Transport Act and, in accordance with the provisions of Article 28f(3), does not determine guilt or liability. 
There were no limits during the investigation that would have a negative impact on its course.  
4. An aggregated description of the technical capabilities and the functions in the 
team of investigators 
The Chairman of the Commission nominated a five-person Investigation Team from among the standing 
members of the Commission with qualifications and competencies regarding the investigation concerned.

## 10. Any other information relevant in the context of the investigation 
Considering that foreign objects that had not been part of railway infrastructure had been found on the track, the 
Investigation Team concluded that they had been placed there deliberately by third parties with the intention of 
causing a railway accident. This conclusion is supported by an occurrence which took place on the same day at 
06.40 hrs on the same track, mainline route Sobolew–Życzyn at km 90.550, which is the subject of a separate 
investigation by the Commission. According to the Investigation Team, preparation and installation of the items, 
i.e. a metal sheet wrapped around the rail and secured beneath the rail foot with a bolt, a mobile phone with a 
connected battery, and additional parts in the form of bolts, as well as signs of damage to track components, on 
track no. 1 at km 115.507, point to deliberate action by third parties. These actions amount to a terrorist act.  
Given the nature of the incident, a detailed investigation into the occurrence, identification of the perpetrators 
and determination of the purpose for which the items were placed on the track fall outside the remit of the 
Investigation Team. The physical evidence and traces recovered by the police will be used by the state security 
authorities to continue the ongoing investigation and produce a detailed explanation of the incident.

# III. DESCRIPTION OF THE OCCURRENCE 
## 1. The occurrence and background information 
### 1.1. Description of the type of occurrence 
The first incident was caused by the theft of YC150-2CS150 overhead contact line involving the cutting out of 
one contact wire on the tensioning cables on tracks no. 1 and no. 2 of mainline route Zarzeka–Puławy Azoty of 
railway line no. 7.. In the occurrence, an electric locomotive with a passenger carriages entered a section of the 
overhead contact line with the contact wire cut out on track no. 1. The occurrence involved PKP Intercity S.A. 
train MPE 83100/1 travelling on the Świnoujście – Rzeszów Główny route, pulled by locomotive EU160-016. 
The second incident involved installation by third parties of a metal sheet on the right-hand rail of track no. 1, 
secured with a bolt beneath the rail under its foot. Furthermore, a smartphone with a power bank was found at 
left-hand rail at km 115.534, propped against the SHP guardrail. A steel bolt was found near the metal sheet

## 2. The factual description of the events 
2.1. The proximate chain of events leading up to the occurrence, including actions taken by 
persons involved, the functioning of rolling stock and technical installations, the 
functioning of the operating system 
On 16 November 2025 at 15:19 hrs, a train driver employed by the railway carrier PKP Intercity S.A. began 
his shift at Lublin Główny station, from where he drove the train to Warszawa Wschodnia station. At 
Warszawa Wschodnia station, the driver took over the driving of train MPE 83100/1 on the Świnoujście–
Rzeszów Główny route. While travelling on track no. 1 of the Sobolew–Życzyn mainline route, in accordance 
with written order "O", he reduced the speed to 20 km/h at the site where the police and other services were 
attending the incident that had occurred at 06.40 hrs. After passing the speed limit are, the driver of the train 
continued travel without noticing any irregularities. He passed Zarzeka station at the scheduled speed and 
continued on track no. 1 on the Zarzeka–Puławy Azoty mainline route.  
On the same day, unknown perpetrators drove up in a car from the direction of track no. 1 on that mainline 
route with the intention of stealing copper cables from the overhead contact line, as evidenced by the car’s tyre 
marks on the roadside between the track and the access road left at the site where the wires were cut out. The 
perpetrators of the theft secured the weights at the anchor masts. To this end, they used sections of reinforcing 
rods, approximately 2.0 m in length, which they had brought to the site; these were secured to the cable of the 
tensioning device with a U-clamp, with the other curved end hooked onto a cable pulley. These rods were 
intended to prevent the weights from falling suddenly when the contact wire was cut. This method of securing 
was used on the side of the anchor masts on tracks no. 1 and 2. Next, one of the two DJP wires between the 
anchor and overlap masts at tracks no. 1 and 2 was cut out. The wires, measuring 2 × 60 m, which had been 
left in the ditch, showed clear signs of mechanical cutting. This occurred at approximately 19:48 hrs, as 
evidenced by the fact that the earlier train, no. 125005, travelling on track no. 1, vacated that track at 19:47 hrs 
without incident, and the driver did not notice any irregularities. The thieves were probably scared off by 
approaching train MPE 83100/1 travelling on Świnoujście–Rzeszów Główny route, which was running 
approximately 10 minutes late and was approaching from Zarzeka station. The driver of that train, travelling 
on track no. 1 at the speed of 159 km/h, noticed at 20:01:11 at km 112.887 a flash on the overhead contact line, 
detected loss of voltage and saw that the automatic dropping device (ADD) on pantograph no. 2 (in the

#                                                  V. CONCLUSIONS 
## 1. A summary of the analysis and conclusions with regard to the causes of the 
occurrence 
An analysis carried out by the Commission’s Investigation Team showed that the rolling stock involved in the 
occurrence, as well as the railway infrastructure, including the overhead contact line, were in good working 
order. Based on the evidence gathered, PKBWK's Investigation Team established the circumstances of the 
occurrence. The occurrence took place as a result of theft of parts of the overhead contact line by unknown 
persons. These persons cut off 60-metre sections of one of the contact wires on tensioning cables from the anchor 
mast to the overlap mast on tracks no. 1 and 2. The DJP contact wire sections were cut at 19:48:29 hrs. This is 
evidenced by the fact that, as recorded by the line's power monitoring equipment, the circuit breakers on tracks 
no. 1 and 2 tripped followed by automatic reclosing. The Investigation Team considered damage to the overhead 
contact line, caused by a section being cut out, as the primary factor contributing to the occurrence. The discarded 
contact wire sections showed clear signs of mechanical cutting and had no burn marks or other damage that 
might indicate that the contact wire was in poor technical condition. The Investigation Team concludes that the 
causal and contributing factors behind the incident involving the severance of the overhead contact line were 
not related to the technical condition of the line, but to interference by third parties. The driver of train MPE 
83100/1, travelling on track no. 1 at 159 km/h, was unable to spot the damage to the contact wire. The video 
footage recovered from locomotive EU160-016 (front view and pantograph view), provided by the carrier, 
confirms that it was not possible to determine the location and nature of the damage to the overhead contact line 
due to limited visibility and the train’s speed of 159 km/h. As the driver did not see the damage to the overhead 
contact line, he did not apply braking before reaching the site of the occurrence, nor did he lower the pantograph, 
and he entered the damaged section at his scheduled speed. The Investigation Team considered the entry of the 
train travelling on track no. 1 into a section where the overhead line had been damaged and sagged as a result of 
theft as the causal factor. A detailed analysis of the evidence showed that the train collided with the sagging DJP 
contact wire at exactly 20:01:11 hrs; this is confirmed by the tripping of the current protection systems, loss of 
voltage and the footage recorded by the locomotive's front view camera. On this basis, the Investigation Team 
revised (compared to the notification received) the kilometre mark and time of the occurrence to km 112.887 
and 20:02 hrs. The exact location of the foreign objects found on the track was also identified. The actual 
kilometre at which the foreign steel element was placed is km 115.507, but the exact time has not been 
established. The Investigation Team determined this time interval to be the period between the last track 
inspection and the date on which these elements were found, i.e. between 8 and 16 November 2025. The 
Investigation Team considered the placement by third parties of the metal sheet on the right-hand rail on track 
no. 1 at km 115.507 as the causal factor of the second incident. The locations and times of the occurrences 
included in the Report are factual and confirmed by the investigation findings, and although they differ from the 
locations indicated in the notification and the decision, they relate to these occurrences.

Team. After the occurrence date, PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe S.A. introduced additional emergency diversions 
using a railway handcar, including on the Zarzeka–Puławy Azoty mainline route.

In the course of the investigation, the Investigation Team identified an external factor relating to the incidents. 
The main factor was interference by third parties. It involved cutting out and attempting to steal the contact wire 
from the overhead contact line on tensioning cables between the anchor and overlap masts. Third parties also 
placed items on the track that were not part of the railway infrastructure. According to the Investigation Team,
