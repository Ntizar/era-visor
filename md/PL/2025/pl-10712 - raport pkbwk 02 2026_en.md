### Type of occurrence: Incident.

# II. THE INVESTIGATION AND ITS CONTEXT 
## 1. Decision to establish an investigation 
The Chairman of the State Commission on Railway Accident Investigation (hereinafter referred to as 
‘PKBWK’ or ‘the Commission’), Mr Tadeusz Ryś, issued Decision No. PKBWK.590.2.2025 dated 20 
August 2025, to initiate proceedings concerning the incident that occurred on 13 August 2025 at 3:43 a.m. 
at the Kraków Główny KGB station, track no. 2, km -0.082, railway line no. 629 Kraków Główny – Kraków 
Bieżanów. Pursuant to the provisions of Article 28e(4) of the Rail Transport Act of 28 March 2003 
(consolidated text: Journal of Laws of 2024, item 697, as amended), hereinafter referred to as the ‘Rail 
Transport Act’, the occurrence was reported to the European Union Railway Agency and registered in its 
database under the number PL-10712. 
Following an analysis of the evidence gathered, the Investigation Team identified the point where the front 
of train PWJ 336210 came to a halt, namely kilometre -0.090 of railway line no. 629, as the scene of the 
incident. 
## 2. Motivation for the decision to establish an investigation 
Based on an analysis of the circumstances, considering the nature of the occurrence, which influences 
railway safety and forms a series of incidents concerning the system as a whole, the Chairman of the 
PKBWK decided to establish an investigation to be conducted by the Commission's Investigation Team 
pursuant to Article 28e(2) of the Rail Transport Act. 
3. The scope and limits of the investigation including a justification thereof, as

the conduct of the investigation or its conclusions 
The investigation into the causal factors and systemic factors contributing to the occurrence was conducted 
under Article 28h(1) of the Rail Transport Act and, in accordance with the provisions  
of Article 28f(3), does not determine guilt or liability. 
There were no limits during the investigation that would have a negative impact on its course.

### 1.1. Railway undertaking(s) or infrastructure manager(s) 
Infrastructure manager PKP PLK S.A. Maintenance-of-Way Department in Kraków 
The tasks of the infrastructure manager, hereinafter referred to as "the operator", include the operation of rail

As part of the supervision of the operation of the rail traffic control equipment, in order to ensure the safety of

Following numerous incidents of malfunctions in the track and turnout vacancy monitoring equipment based on

causes of these malfunctions. From 2022, it corresponded with the equipment manufacturer ALSTOM ZWUS

and did not take any further corrective action.

### 3.1. Human and individual characteristics 
Employer POLREGIO S.A., The Małopolska Department in Kraków ensured that the driver operating the PWJ

box, in accordance with the schedule, he had taken over the duties of the assistant train dispatcher at 6.00 p.m., 
following a 24-hour rest period. At midnight, in accordance with the provisions of the technical regulations of 
the ‘KGB’ signal box, the assistant train dispatcher took over the duties of the train dispatcher. The occurrence 
took place during the 10th hour of his night shift and the 4th hour of his duties as a train dispatcher. The nature 
of the work requires the employee to maintain constant concentration and be present at his workstation at all 
times, as train traffic and shunting operations take place continuously, which contributes to his fatigue – a factor

that the Investigation Team identified as one of the contributing factors. Breaks are permitted only to attend to 
physiological needs and to rest, and only at times when there is no train traffic, as set out in the timetable. The 
workstations of the train dispatcher and assistant train dispatcher must be staffed around the clock. 
In order for employees to take the break provided for under the Labour Code, one of them leaves his workstation 
and the other takes over his duties, while continuing to carry out his own. This was the situation at the time of 
the occurrence. Working under stress and the fatigue of the staff at the ‘KGB’ signal box had adverse effects, 
leading to an error, a decline in the efficiency of their work, and a reduction in the safety of train traffic and 
shunting operations. Consequently, the Investigation Team deemed it appropriate to review the staffing levels

employee possessed the required qualifications and had been cleared by the occupational health service to carry 
out the duties of a train dispatcher. His mental and physical condition raised no concerns for the railway 
commission. 
### 3.2. Job factors 
The rail vehicles involved in the occurrence had the relevant approvals for operation on the railway network in 
Poland and were in good working order. The drivers' workstations were typical and did not contribute to the 
occurrence. 
Work as a train dispatcher at the ‘KGB’ signal box involved a very high level of responsibility for the safety of 
passengers and train traffic, and requires the employee’s physical and mental condition. The employee must 
carry out his duties relating to train traffic and shunting operations at the station and on adjacent routes on a shift 
basis, including at night. This involves a great deal of mental strain and stress, and even a momentary lapse in 
concentration can have negative consequences. The Kraków Główny station is characterised by a high volume 
of passenger train traffic (around 600 trains per day) and the resulting additional shunting operations, which 
involve changing locomotives, uncoupling and coupling carriages to and from trains, etc. The nature of this 
work involves a large number of responsibilities and requires staff to work continuously in front of a computer 
screen, both as the train dispatcher and assistant train dispatcher; in a 12-hour shift system, particularly during 
night shifts, this has an undeniable impact on their fatigue, even when the railway traffic control equipment is 
functioning efficiently. 
The frequent malfunctions of the counter-based system for detecting tracks and turnout vacancy further prolong 
the time taken to set the route for trains, require a substitute signal (Sz) to be displayed on the signalling device 
or a written order to be issued, result in speed restrictions for trains and contributes to the occurrence and 
accumulation of train delays. 
The Risk Register compiled by PKP PLK S.A. identifies the following risks under the following numbers: 2.10.4 
‘malfunction of track vacancy monitoring equipment’ and 2.10.5 ‘malfunction of turnout vacancy 
monitoring equipment’. In the opinion of the Risk Assessment Team, the above-mentioned failures could result 
in a serious railway accident; accordingly, safety measures and requirements have been identified. In this case, 
in accordance with the applicable instructions, the process of resetting the axis counts in the axis counter section 
was carried out. If this procedure proved ineffective, the automation technician would proceed to reset the track-
side electronics, and the Department would notify the manufacturer of the railway traffic control equipment of 
the type and nature of the failure. On the day of the occurrence, the axle counters in sections Iz419 and Iz411/417 
at the Kraków Główny station malfunctioned . Despite the process of resetting the track counters after each train 
had passed, the railway traffic control equipment continued to indicate that the track was occupied. The staff at

technicians  
later. This notification was made at 03:36 a.m., which, in the opinion of the Investigation Team, was too late

The incident took place on 13 August 2025 at 03:43 am. at the Kraków Główny station. The causal factor 
identified by the Investigation Team was that ‘the route was set by the train dispatcher at the Kraków Główny 
station, diverting the train from the main line track no. 2Z (route 629) onto station track 2 and setting the 
substitute signal ‘Sz’ on the entry semaphore Z42 for train no. PWJ 336210, despite the fact that this track was 
occupied by train n o. MHE 53171, which was parked at platform 3’. 
On the basis of the entries in logbook E-1758, the Investigation Team concluded that at 00:07 a.m., following 
the passage of the MHE 35170 train, monitoring of the vacancy status of sections Iz419 and Iz411/417 was 
stopped, which was identified as one of the factors contributing to the occurrence. The train dispatcher carried 
out the process of resetting the axle count in the axle counter section each time before a train or railway vehicles 
passed through a section showing an occupancy. Despite carrying out these steps in accordance with the 
instructions, the failure of the axle counters persisted. 
The frequent malfunctions of the counter-based system for detecting tracks and turnout vacancy at the Kraków 
Główny station since 2022 further prolong the time taken to set the route for trains, require a substitute signal 
(Sz) to be displayed on the signalling device or a written order to be issued, result in speed restrictions for trains 
and contributes to the occurrence and accumulation of train delays. The infrastructure manager continuously 
monitored any faults occurring in this equipment. It corresponded and held meetings with the equipment 
manufacturer ALSTOM ZWUS Sp. z o.o. and with the railway undertakings RegioJet a.s. and ‘PKP Intercity’ 
S.A., whose rolling stock was causing disruptions to the operation of the axle counters. The above-mentioned 
railway undertakings declared their willingness cooperate and implemented measures aimed at identifying the 
sources and causes of malfunctions in track-side equipment. As a corrective measure, ‘PKP Intercity’ S.A. took 
the identified rolling stock equipped with static converters out of service on the PKP network. However, the 
other railway undertaking did not take the rolling stock out of service and, following the tests, the manufacturer 
of the railway traffic control devices simply concluded that its devices complied with the standards set for the 
Polish market and did not take any further corrective action.  
Frequent axle counter failures continue to occur at the Kraków Główny station, and the Investigation Team 
identified these as one of the contributing factors. As the vast majority of failures that occur are one-off in nature 
and are resolved once the counters are reset, the staff at the ‘KGB’ signal box decided to notify the automation 
technicians 
later.

which the Investigation Team identified as another contributing factor. 
Prior to the occurrence, the train dispatcher was also carrying out the duties of the assistant train dispatcher, who

himself, he also carried out procedures designed to prevent a train from entering an occupied track, i.e. he made

in front of computer screens, with no opportunity to take a longer break. The employee’s fatigue was caused by

equipment. The Team identified the above human, work-related and organisational factors as contributing to the 
incident; no environmental or systemic factors were identified.

## 3. Additional comments 
During the course of its investigation, the Investigation Team identified the following irregularities, which were 
not causal, systemic or contributing factors in the occurrence: 
- recurring interference with the ELS-95 axle counters, since 2022, on the premises of PKP PLK S.A., 
Maintenance-of-Way Department in Kraków, due to rolling stock passing over the sensors and causing 
electromagnetic interference levels to exceed the permitted limits; 
- significant interference was found in the sensors. which was adversely affecting their proper operation; 
- the axle counters were not immune to interference caused by exceeding the permissible levels of 
electromagnetic interference, as specified in the technical documentation for the ELS-95 wheel sensor(‘passage 
of rolling stock generating interference in the frequency range of 27kHz-52kHz at levels exceeding those 
permitted  
in technical specification CLC/TS 50238-3 may cause the wheel sensor to malfunction’). 
- the operating instruction for the local EBI SCREEN 300 workstation at the Kraków Główny station does not 
include a description of how to use the SZ command when the route is correctly configured and contains vacancy 
monitoring sections for which a reset has previously been carried out (these sections are awaiting the first control 
run by rolling stock, and the points on the route are operating correctly), together with visualisation examples.

# VI. SAFETY RECOMMENDATIONS 
1) PKP PLK S.A. The Maintenance-of-Way Department in Kraków must set up a team comprising a 
representative of the Department, an occupational health and safety inspector and a community labour 
inspector. The aim of the team’s work will be to carry out a workload assessment (time study) for the train 
dispatcher and assistant train dispatcher at the ‘KGB’ signal tower; on the basis of this, the Department 
must adjust staffing levels and the division of duties within the train dispatcher team at a signal box 
accordingly. 
2) RegioJet a.s., which operates vehicles of the B2xx, Bc2xx, Bc1xx, Bc3xx and Bm1xx series, must take the 
necessary corrective measures to eliminate malfunctions in the axle counters and ensure that these vehicles 
are fully compliant with the essential requirements of the railway system. 
3) ‘PKP Intercity’ S.A., which operates vehicles of the Apm61 (Panorama) series, must take the necessary 
corrective measures to eliminate malfunctions in the axle counters and ensure that these vehicles are fully 
compliant with the essential requirements of the railway system. 
4) ALSTOM Polska S.A. must amend the provisions of the ‘operating manual for the EBI SCREEN 300 
railway traffic control system’ for the Kraków Główny station (KGB and KGA signal boxes) regarding the 
use of the SZ command when the route is correctly set, including sections subject to vacancy monitoring to 
which a reset command has previously been sent (the sections are awaiting the first control run-through by 
rolling stock, and the points on the route are operating correctly), together with visualisation examples in 
accordance with the provisions of Instructions Ie-20 and Ie-20a. These changes will form the basis for the 
infrastructure manager to update the operating instructions for the local EBI SCREEN 300 terminal  
at the Kraków Główny station. 
5) PKP PLK S.A. The Maintenance-of-Way Department in Kraków, in collaboration with the manufacturer 
of railway traffic control equipment, must take steps to gradually replace the ELS-95 wheel sensors with 
new-generation sensors. 
6) PKP PLK S.A. The Maintenance-of-Way Department in Kraków, in collaboration with the manufacturer 
of railway traffic control equipment, must take steps to reduce risks to railway safety by implementing 
changes to the railway traffic control equipment in order to eliminate ‘unconditional substitute signals 
without dependencies’ at the Kraków Główny station. 
7) The Chairman of the UTK must consider setting up a team of experts tasked with gathering opinions and 
knowledge to help eliminate instances of interference with ELS-95 axle counters by rolling stock.
