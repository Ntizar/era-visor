# I. 
# SUMMARY 
### Type of Event: Serious accident. 
Description: An occurrence on a Cat. A level crossing, on-site operated, at km 90.472 
of railway line no. 18 Kutno – Piła Główna, located at Aleksandrów 
Kujawski station. In the occurrence, a Mercedes Sprinter van (hereinafter 
referred to as "the van") drove in front of passenger train ROJ 55575 
operated on the Włocławek – Toruń Główny route by carrier POLREGIO 
S.A., Kujawsko-Pomorski Railway Line Plant in Bydgoszcz, which was 
entering the station. which was entering the station. The van left the access 
road turning left into voivodeship road DW266 on which the Cat. a level 
crossing is located. The driver of the van entered the opposite traffic lane 
after crossing the P4 line while the barrier booms were being raised and 
continued onto the level crossing, despite the S2 traffic signal giving a red 
flashing light. The driver of the train, entering Aleksandrów Kujawski 
station, noticed the van negotiating the level crossing and implemented 
emergency braking at the speed of 117.6 km/h and gave the Rp1 
"Attention" signal. Despite the emergency braking, when the cab of the 
van was in the axis of track 101, it was struck by the coupling of passenger 
train ROJ 55575. As a result of the impact, the van was thrown to the left 
side of the train, caught fire, rolled over and came to a stop on its right 
side 62 m from the centreline axis of the crossing. After the impact, the 
van was turned by an angle of approx. 200° to its direction of travel. The 
cab with the driver burnt down. The car wreckage was located at turnout 
7a/b on track 104 at km 90.534 of railway line no. 18. After the 
occurrence, the head of the train came to a stop at km 90.845 at 
Aleksandrów Kujawski station. 
### Date of the occurrence: 5 May 2025, 08:40 hrs. 
Location of the occurrence: Railway line no. 18 Kutno – Piła Główna, Aleksandrów Kujawski station, 
track 101, on-site operated Cat. A level crossing, km 90.472; crossing 
identification number 018 090 472; geographical location 52°52'01.6"N 
18°42'26.9"E. 
Consequences of the occurrence: As a result of the serious accident, the driver of the van died on the spot. 
The van was completely destroyed and its cargo was partially destroyed. 
There was also damage to the first section of railway vehicle 22WEg-018 
and to the railway infrastructure.

# II. THE INVESTIGATION AND ITS CONTEXT 
## 1. Decision to establish an investigation 
The Chairman of the State Commission on Railway Accident Investigation (hereinafter referred to as 
"PKBWK" or "the Commission") Mr. Tadeusz Ryś issued decision no. PKBWK.590.1.2025 of 7 May 2025 
establish an investigation into the circumstances of the serious accident that occurred on 5 May 2025 at 
08:40 hrs on the Cat. A level crossing at Aleksandrów Kujawski station, track no. 101, km 90.472 of railway 
line No. 18. Pursuant to the provisions of Article 28e(4) of the Act of 28 March 2003 on rail transport 
(consolidated text: Journal of Laws of 2024, item 697, as amended), hereinafter referred to as the "Rail 
Transport Act", the occurrence was reported to the European Union Railway Agency and registered in its 
database under the number PL-10680. 
## 2. Motivation for the decision to establish an investigation 
The occurrence meets the definition of a serious railway accident as referred to in Article 4(46) of the Rail 
Transport Act. Taking into account that the occurrence meets the criteria of a "serious railway accident" as 
referred to in Article 4(46) of the Rail Transport Act, the Chairman of PKBWK decided that an investigation 
should be undertaken by the Commission's Investigation Team under Article 28e(1) of the Rail Transport 
Act. 
3. The scope and limits of the investigation including a justification thereof, as 
well as an explanation of any delay that is considered a risk or other impact 
to the conduct of the investigation or its conclusions 
The investigation into the circumstances and causal, contributing and systemic factors of the occurrence 
was conducted under Article 28h(1) of the Rail Transport Act which, in accordance with the provisions of 
Article 28f(3), does not determine guilt or liability. 
There were no limits during the investigation that would have a negative impact on its course. 
## 4. An aggregated description of the technical capabilities and the functions in 
the team of investigators 
The Chairman of the Commission appointed the Investigation Team from among the standing members of 
the Commission with qualifications and competencies regarding the investigation concerned. 
## 5. A description of the communication and consultation process established with 
persons or entities involved in the occurrence during the investigation and in 
relation to the information provided 
Under Article 28h(2)(5) of the Rail Transport Act, the Chairman of PKBWK obliged specific persons from 
the railway commission to cooperate with the Investigation Team (Letter no. PKBWK. 590.1.2025 of 7 
May 2025). 
On 9 May 2025, the chairman of the railway commission formally submitted the documentation gathered 
by the railway commission to the head of the Commission's Investigation Team.

## 7. A description of the investigation methods and techniques as well as analysis 
methods applied to establish the facts and findings referred to in the report 
In the course of the investigation, the Investigation Team took into account the provisions of national rules, 
internal regulations and technical documentation of the infrastructure manager, i.e. PKP PLK S.A., and the 
railway operator POLREGIO S.A. Furthermore, the Investigation Team relied on their own knowledge and 
experience, as well as on the documentation gathered by the Investigation Team, the railway commission 
and the prosecutor's office.

 
an inspection of the place of the occurrence, including an inspection of the level crossing, the station 
and the voivodeship and access roads, 
 
measurements at the crossing (warning times, location of traffic signs), 
 
an analysis of the visibility of the crossing from a road vehicle, 
 
production of photographic and video documentation, 
 
an analysis of the documentation submitted by the railway carrier, rail infrastructure manager and 
road manager, 
 
an analysis of the material gathered and made available by the prosecutor's office, 
 
an inspection of the level crossing concerned, 
 
interviews with the persons involved in the occurrence, 
 
an analysis of recordings from the city surveillance system, the railway vehicle and a witness's road 
vehicle, 
 
an analysis of recordings from voice communications exchanged through the announcing link and 
over the train radio network, 
 
an analysis of the data from the event data recorder of rail vehicle 22WEg-018 "Elf", 
 
a post-occurrence inspection of rail vehicle 22WEg-018 "Elf", and of the road vehicle involved in 
the occurrence.

### 3.2. Job factors 
§1(3)(2) of the Instruction Ir-7 for the operation of level crossings and passages provide that the instruction 
applies, inter alia, to "workers who additionally perform activities related to the operation of the crossing". The 
term "crossing keeper" as used in the Report in the light of the above should also be understood as the position 
of "a pointsman", whose duties include the operation of the Cat. A level crossing at km 90.472. 
According to the provisions of §9(1) of the aforementioned instruction, the crossing keeper should be constantly 
vigilant for the safety of railway traffic and be ready to close the barriers when a rail vehicle approaches the 
operated crossing, and should give the D8 signal in the direction of the rail vehicle using a hand-held torch with 
a white light. The D8 signal means "Crossing keeper present at the level crossing or passage". He or she should 
also observe the area around the level crossing being operated. In the opinion of the Investigation Team, the 
organisation of the pointsman's workstation at the "P1" post operating the the barrier devices does not provide 
for meeting of the above provisions due to the layout of the rooms, the thickness of the external wall and the 
window sills protruding inwards. The pointsman only has a view of tracks 101, 102 and 114 just in front of the 
frontage of the building and of the station towards the "AK" box and level crossing. The construction of a 
partition wall dividing one room into two, i.e. the pointsman's room and the staff room, resulted in the windows 
being obscured, making it impossible to observe the traffic situation from the direction of Nieszawa Waganiec 
station at all times. Such a state of workplace organisation has existed since 1997, when the function of the 
technical post was changed from a command box to an end-of-train statement (skp) post, from where the worker 
operating the level crossing would confirm that the train had entered correctly. The skp equipment informed the 
personnel, by means of a lit indicator lamp, of a train coming from the direction of Nieszawa Waganiec (with a 
properly functioning line block). The information obtained through the skp device was helpful to the workers, 
despite the lack of visibility of the adjacent route. 
In 2020, equipment for automatic block posts (APO) with track occupancy control were installed on the 
Nieszawa Waganiec - Aleksandrów Kujawski route, which resulted in the elimination of the skp, and the 
function of the post was changed to a "P1" switch post with crossing operation without auxiliary equipment 
informing of incoming trains. 
The pointsman operates the crossing from the signal box and closes and opens the barrier booms only on the 
basis of telephone calls from the signaller and acknowledgement of their closure. The pointsman had not been 
provided with other means, e.g. radio-telephone (in the train or shunting network) or a crossing keeper support 
system device (SWDP), proximity signalling, viewing system for the invisible area(obscured by the wall), etc.

### 3.3. Organisational factors and assignments 
The scope of activities performed at the pointsman's workstation at the "P1" post is defined by the technical 
regulations of Aleksandrów Kujawski station. The duties, work and conduct rules for the safe performance of

## Página 41

According to §11(6) of instruction Ir-7, before opening the barriers, the worker operating them should make 
sure that no other train is expected to pass on any track. The worker could do so despite the lack of visibility 
from the direction of the moving train, by checking the entries in the R-142 logbook. 
Furthermore, according to §11(4) of the aforementioned instruction, during the passage of a rail vehicle, the 
crossing keeper should stand in the place designated by the crossing regulations and give the signal D8 using a 
hand-held torch with a white light, so that the signal is visible to the train of the approaching train or the driver 
of the rail vehicle. It is not possible to give the D8 signal at the "P1" post so that it is visible to the drivers driving 
trains from the direction of Nieszawa Waganiec, and the rules of operation of the level crossing do not take this 
into account. For this reason, not giving the D8 signal had been adopted by the staff and supervision as a regular 
practice. The infrastructure manager had not worked out or taken any actions to enable this signal to be given, 
and train drivers have accepted this and had not reacted to the absence of the D8 signal. The Investigation Team 
decided not to issue recommendations in this regard due to the ongoing modernisation of the equipment and 
decommissioning of the "P1" post. 
§12(6)(5)of the signalling instruction Ie-1 (E-1) requires the train driver to give the Rp 1 "Standby" signal after 
passing the W 6b indicator. Such an indicator was set up on track no. 1 of the Nieszawa Waganiec - Aleksandrów 
Kujawski route at km 89.672 and means that "... behind the indicator there is a level crossing or a passage 
equipped with a semi-automatic or automatic crossing system, without any connection or dependence in the the

The Rp1 signal was given by the train driver when he noticed the van entering the crossing. The driver of train 
ROJ 55575 started to give the Rp1 "Attention" signal 2s before the collision, being 55m from the axis of the 
crossing.

### 5. Previous occurrences of a similar character 
As part of the investigation, the Investigation Team analysed selected occurrences handled by PKBWK that 
took place under similar circumstances at level crossings between 2015 and 2025. A brief description of the 
occurrences and their consequences. 
1. On 11 July 2015 at 17:10 hrs, on a Cat. A level crossing at km 23.506 of the Gałkówek - Koluszki route 
of line no. 17 Łódź Fabryczna - Koluszki, a train ran into road vehicles (passenger cars) present on the 
crossing during the opening of the barriers. The barriers were opened by the crossing keeper after one 
train had passed on track no. 2 although another train was approaching on track no. 1, which wass notified 
to and acknowledged by the keeper (via the SCP-2 system). The premature entry of the passenger cars 
onto the crossing occurred while the barriers were being raised and the signal prohibiting entry beyond 
traffic signals was being given. 
As a result of the occurrence, one person (a car passenger) died on the spot, one person (a car driver) died 
after being taken to hospital, and furthermore a child (a car passenger) was injured and taken to hospital. 
In the course of the investigation, the following causes were established, among others: 
- no function to keep the barriers in the closed position for the odd direction, 
- inadequate ergonomics of the crossing keeper's workstation. 
The established causes are consistent with the occurrence described in this Report. 
2. On 26 March 2016, at 07:37 hrs, at the Cat. A level crossing located at the Dziarnowo junction, the 
Dziarnowo - Inowrocław Towarowy route, track no. 1, km 95.669 of railway line no. 353 Poznań Wschód 
- Skandawa, a rail vehicle ran into a road vehicle (a Audi A4 KOMBI passenger car) which entered the 
crossing with barriers opened directly in front of the oncoming train of the carrier "PKP Intercity" S.A. 
There were two adults in the car who died on the spot. 
The PKBWK Accident Team concluded that the cause of the serious accident was the failure to close the 
barriers by the signaller at Dziarnowo junction before the permissive "S2" aspect was given the exit signal 
"C½". 
In the course of the investigation, the following cause was established, among others: 
- failure to make the aspect of the "C½" signal conditional on the closure of the barriers, which is 
consistent with the occurrence described in this Report. 
3. On 4 April 2017, at 17:34 hrs, on the Cat. A level crossing located at km 244.676 on the Zawadówka – 
Uherka junction, track no. 1, railway line no. 7: Warszawa Wschodnia Osobowa - Dorohusk, a rail 
vehicle ran into a road vehicle (a Toyota Yaris passenger car), which had entered the Cat. A level 
crossing with the barriers opened directly in front of the oncoming passenger train. The root cause of 
the occurrence was the failure of the crossing keeper to close the barriers in accordance with the barrier 
closing times provided in the "Crossing operation rules" despite receiving a notification from the 
signaller at Zawadowka station. 
In the course of the investigation, the following indirect causes were identified:  
- Failure of the driver of the Toyota Yaris passenger car to exercise special caution, in particular by 
entering the level crossing without making sure that no rail vehicle was approaching, in conflict with 
the provisions of Article 19(1) of the Act of 20 June 1997 on the Road Traffic Law.  
- Absence of the crossing keeper at a place from which he should have observed the passing train and 
given the D8 signal ("Keeper present at the crossing") while holding the signal instruments as stated in 
the "Regulations for the operation of level crossings or pedestrian passages".  
- The fact that the crossing keeper carried out work despite lack of concentration as a result of a difficult 
situation not related to his job. 
Furthermore, between 2020 and 2025 (up to the date of the occurrence), a total of 18 Category B-18 accidents 
were recorded that involved the failure to close barrier booms or premature opening of barrier booms at Cat. 
A crossings.

# V. CONCLUSIONS 
## 1. A summary of the analysis and conclusions with regard to the causes of the 
occurrence 
The analysis carried out by the Commission's Investigation Team showed that the rolling stock and road 
vehicle involved in the in the serious accident, as well as the elements of the railway infrastructure, including 
the semi-automatic level crossing system installed at the crossing, were technically serviceable. 
By raising the barrier booms to the vertical position, the worker allowed the road vehicles to enter the level 
crossing, despite the fact that a passenger train was approaching, which was considered by the Investigation 
Team to be one of the one of the causal factors. Another causal factor identified by the Team was the entry of 
the van onto a Cat. level crossing when the traffic lights prohibited entry beyond the S2 traffic signal. 
The Investigation Team identified the following actions as factors contributing to the occurrence: 
- After the operations at the level crossing were completed, the employee operating the crossing proceeded to 
raise the barrier booms despite having previously acknowledged the entry of train ROJ 55575 and making 
corresponding entries in the operations logbook (R-142) concerning the entry of train ROJ 55575. 
- The driver of the van joining the traffic to left towards the level crossing, traversing the P4 line which 
prohibited such a manoeuvre. 
- Failure of the van driver to take special caution when entering and negotiating the level crossing. The 
behaviour of the passenger car driver coming from the opposite direction and exercising appropriate vigilance 
and caution ensured that the vehicle could be stopped and a collision with the train could be avoided. The van 
driver did not observe the surrounding and did not respond to the sound signals given by the train horn, as a 
result of which he failed to brake the road vehicle. He treated the raising of the barriers while the lights on the 
signal were still on as fulfilling the conditions for safe driving through the level crossing. 
- Inappropriate organisation of the workstation for the operator of the level crossing, preventing proper 
observation of trains and shunting operations conducted from the direction of Nieszawa Waganiec station. 
Furthermore, other measures were not ensured, e.g. a radiotelephone in the train and shunting network or an 
SWDP device, proximity signalling or observation of the area not visible (obscured by the wall). 
- No signage at the junction, i.e. the connection between the access road and the public road. 
As the systemic factor, the Investigation Team identified the failure of the infrastructure manager, i.e. PKP 
PLK S.A., to take the appropriate measures indicated in recommendation 3a of Report . PKBWK/01/2017, 
despite identification of a risk of an accident on the level crossing due to the lack of linking of the semi-automatic 
traffic system at the level crossing with the station's system, and despite including that risk in Point 5.11.31 of 
the Hazard Record. 
## 2. Measures taken since the occurrence 
1. The train speed limit of 40 km/h has been introduced at turnout no. 5 of Aleksandrów Kujawski station due 
to the horizontal unevenness. 
2. In order to prevent road vehicles from joining the traffic and entering the level crossing, the entry to the 
access road was blocked with provisional barriers by the Toruń Główny Operations Section. 
## 3. Additional comments 
In the course of the post-occurrence inspection, the Investigation Team identified also the following other 
irregularities:

# VI. SAFETY RECOMMENDATIONS 
### Recommendations and their addressees: 
### Voivodeship Roads Authority in Bydgoszcz shall: 
1. Renovate the horizontal signage of the P-4 and P-14 lines on the access road to the crossing, plus complete the

4. Until the signalling equipment at Aleksandrów Kujawski station is modernised, link the semi-automatic traffic

ensured, take appropriate measures to minimise the risk of occurrences at the crossings.
