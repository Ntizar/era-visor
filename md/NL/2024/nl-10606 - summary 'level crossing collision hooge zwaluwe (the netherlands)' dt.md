# The Dutch Safety 

# Board
Safe living, safe working, safety. It sounds obvious, but safety 
cannot be guaranteed. In the Netherlands, high-quality 
knowledge and technology, and sophisticated procedures 
are used to keep society safe. Yet, serious accidents or even 
disasters can still happen. The Dutch Safety Board investigates 
occurrences and draws lessons from them. In this way, we 
contribute to safer procedures and organization. And thus to the 
Netherlands as a safer country.
The Dutch Safety Board mainly focuses on situations in which 
people depend on third parties for their personal safety, such 
as the government or companies. In our investigations we do 
not address issues of blame or liability. The reports of the Safety 
Board are public. Section 69 of the Dutch Safety Board Act 
prohibits the use of the Safety Board's investigation information in 
criminal, disciplinary or civil proceedings.
N.B. This report is published in the Dutch language, with a translation of 
the summary and recommendations in English. If there is a difference in 
interpretation between the Dutch and English version, the Dutch text will 
prevail.

The railway company was unaware of the risks on this section of

them. Train drivers must be able to report safety risks, and the 
railway company must actively collect reports. These reports must 
make a contribution to improving safety. We therefore make the
