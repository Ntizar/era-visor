## Página 2

This is an interim statement with an update on the progress of the Dutch Safety
NL EN
Board’s investigation of the railway accident that occurred near Voorschoten
station in the early morning of Tuesday 4 April 2023. In this accident a road-rail
excavator was hit by a freight train and subsequently by a passenger train. The
road-rail excavator had been carrying out work during scheduled maintenance
works.
## Research questions
The investigation focuses on answering the following questions:
• How could the excavator leave its workplace, drive onto an in-service track and
then collide with the freight train and then the passenger train?
• How are occupational and railway safety considered in the planning and
execution of working on the tracks while train traffic continues? How is this
supervised internally and externally?
## Planning
The report of the study is in its final phase, publication is expected mid-May 2024
on the research page.
