Damages of rolling stock: 
One turnout transport wagon and the following wagon carrying turnout equipment

normaaliin käyttöön raiteet saatiin yhdeksän päivän kuluttua. 
Disturbances of traffic: 
The accident significantly hindered traffic for one day. The tracks were returned to 
full normal use after nine days.
