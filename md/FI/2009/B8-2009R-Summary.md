SUMMARY 
FATAL LEVEL CROSSING ACCIDENT IN SEINÄJOKI, FINLAND, ON 3 DECEMBER 
2009 
On Thursday 3 December 2009 at 4.05 a.m., a fatal level crossing accident occurred at the Teppo level 
crossing in Seinäjoki. The level crossing was equipped with half barriers. A passenger train en route to 
Helsinki collided with a car which was at a halt on the crossing. The collision caused immediate to the 
car driver. 
The cause of the accident lay in the train colliding with the car, which had come to a halt on the 
crossing. Due to issues that became apparent during the investigation, the investigation commission 
will not issue any recommendations as a result of the accident.
