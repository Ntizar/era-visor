a departure of the long distance passenger train No. 350 without
•
regular dispatching of the train.
Contributory factor:
an absence of the technical equipment which prevents a train from
•
passing a signal in case of danger.
Underlying cause:
failure to comply with the technological procedures of the IM for
•
departure of the train from the operating point – failure to request

departure signal device in case when the signal of the main
departure signal device is not visible from the place where the train
stopped.

none.
