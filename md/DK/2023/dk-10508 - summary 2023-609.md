Summary case 2023-609 
On Saturday 9th, a regional train from Næstved towards Copenhagen H., hit a person in a level crossing. 
The person died as a result of the collision. 
The level crossing 115, was located on Banedanmark's infrastructure, south of Køge Station. 
The train were equipped with the train control system ETCS, and the level crossing was an automatically 
secured crossing with full barriers. 
Data showed that the speed of the train shortly before the crossing was 54 km/h. 
The level crossing was secured. 
Just before the train passed the level crossing, the driver became aware of a person crossing the tracks. 
The train was braked immediately, but the collision was unavoidable. 
Conclusion 
The cause of the accident was that the deceased intentionally crossed the barriers at the level crossing.   
The person was under the influence of alcohol and probably had headphones in the ears. 
Recommendations 
After carrying out the above investigation and evaluating the available data, the accident commission finds 
no basis for issuing safety recommendations.
