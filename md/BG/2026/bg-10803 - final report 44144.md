### 1. Summary 
### 1.1. Brief description of the occurrence 
On 17.04.2026 at 15:50h, fast train (FT) No. 9623 departed from Ruse station made up of 2 
coaches, 8 axles, 80 tonnes, hauled by electric locomotive No. 91520044144-1, with a train driver, an 
assistant train driver and an on-board crew consisting of a train manager and a conductor. The train runs 
daily to timetable from Ruse to Varna. It is operated with rolling stock and staff of the railway undertaking 
“BDZ – Passenger Services” EOOD. 
FT No. 9623 arrived at Visoka Polyana station at 17:48h and departed for Hitrino station at 17:49. 
Train movement between Visoka Polyana and Hitrino stations was secured by the telephone block method 
owing to a fault that had occurred in the semi-automatic block system. 
Enroute from Visoka Polyana station, the train driver set the locomotive controller to the neutral 
(zero) position as the train was running on a falling gradient, at which point the locomotive’s main circuit 
breaker tripped. The train driver initiated emergency braking of the train with the train brake at 103+000 
km on the Visoka Polyana – Hitrino open line and stopped at 17:55h. The train driver and the assistant 
train driver inspected the locomotive and noticed smoke and fire in the machine compartment, coming out 
of the contacts of the autotransformer tap-changer (ATC). The fire was put out using the three fire 
extinguishers onboard the locomotive. The voltage in the overhead catenary was not switched off.  
The train manager of FT No. 9623 informed the relevant services of the fire by mobile telephone. 
At 18:05h, the duty traffic controller at Hitrino station notified the train dispatcher that fire occurred 
onboard the locomotive and that FT No. 9623 stopped on the open line. The train manager reported: “the 
fire has been extinguished, but the locomotive is damaged and cannot continue.” 
At 18:32, the train dispatcher issued instructions to close the Visoka Polyana – Hitrino open line 
for movement of all trains.

departed from Visoka Polyana station to haul Fast train (FT) No. 9623. At 19:19h the fast train was brought 
into Visoka Polyana station.

### 1.2. Place and time of the occurre  
 Between Visoka Polyana and Hitrino stations, at 103+000 km, at 17:55h, while running, a fire 
occurred onboard electric locomotive No. 91520044144-1 operating FT No. 9623. The train driver stopped 
the train on the open line, and the fire was put out on the spot.  
### 1.3. Causal, contributing and systemic factors 
### Causal factor: 
- A worn cam on contactor S3 of the contactor block of the autotransformer tap-changer (ATC). It 
did not allow the contacts to open reliably. As a result, the current circuit remained closed and the tap-
changer short-circuited. 
### Contributing factor: 
- The planned repair of the locomotive did not include the necessary diagnostic and repair works on 
the ATC in accordance with the Rules on the Overhaul of Series 44 Electric Locomotives. As a result, the 
ATC operated longer mileage without repair, which affected the reliability of the unit and of the locomotiv. 
### Systemic factors: 
The investigating commission established that: 
- The railway undertaking did not comply with the provisions of the applicable regulatory 
framework regarding the maintenance of Series 44 and 45 locomotives that have undergone major 
overhaul and are in daily service. The repair intervals were not observed as prescribed. 
- The railway undertaking does not keep accurate records and statistics of the units fitted to different 
locomotives under the unit-exchange repair method, which allows units to be fitted to locomotives without 
having undergone diagnostics and repair in accordance with the regulatory framework and in line with 
their actual mileage, regardless of how many locomotives they had previously been fitted onto. 
- The railway undertaking has not introduced training programmes for the repair and operating staff 
regarding the quality of repair works for the servicing of the locomotives. 
### 1.4. Direct causes and consequences of the occurrence. 
Analysing the findings and the information gathered after the accident, the documents provided, 
and the results of the interviews with the staff involved, the investigating commission established breaches 
in the relevant factors that contributed to the occurrence.  
Taking into account the results and measurements carried out on the locomotive after the accident 
and the causal and contributing factors as defined in Regulation (EU) 2020/572, the investigating 
commission established that the fire onboard locomotive No. 91520044144-1 was caused by improper 
maintenance and repair. 
On the transition from traction mode to coasting, the S3 contacts remained in an intermediate 
position and the current flowing between them was not interrupted, which prevented the breaking of the 
electric arc and led to a rise in temperature and the two contacts catching fire.

### 2. Разследване 
### 2.1. Решение за започване на разследване. 
Решение за започване на разследване по безопасността е взето от члена на управителния 
съвет на НБРПВВЖТ в Република България, ръководител на разследване на железопътни 
произшествия и инциденти в съответствие на чл. 22, параграф 3 от Директива (ЕС) 2016/798 на 
Европейския парламент и Съвета. Предвид тежестта на произшествието и неговото въздействие 
върху безопасността в железопътния транспорт, разследването е ориентирано основно към 
установяване на причините и анализ, целящ подобряване на безопасността и предотвратяването на 
други произшествия (пожари в локомотиви серии 44 и 45). 
### 2.2. Мотиви на решението за започване на разследване. 
Членът на Управителния съвет на НБРПВВЖТ на основание чл. 20, параграф 2, (а) и (в) от 
Директива (ЕС) 2016/798, чл. 115к, ал. 1, т. 2 от ЗЖТ и чл. 76, ал. 1, т. 2 от Наредба № 59 от 5.12.2006 
г., е взел решение за започване разследване на произшествието.  
Разследването е предприето предвид обстоятелствата и рисковете застрашаващи живота и 
здравето на обслужващия персонал и пътниците в пътническия влак от възникналия пожар в 
електрически локомотив № 91520044144-1, обслужвал БВ № 9623 на 17.04.2026 г. във време на 
движение на влака в междугарието Висока поляна – Хитрино. 
### 2.3. Обхват и ограничения на разследването. 
В обхвата на разследването са включени и анализирани организационният и човешки 
фактори, Системата за управление на безопасността, относима към ремонта и поддръжката,

 Огледът на контакторният блок на автотрансформатора на превключвателя на степени

на гърбичните шайби. И двата контакта са подвижни и имат Г-образна форма. Изработени от мед 
и сребро които имат добра електропроводимост, топлопроводимост и приемлива твърдост. Медта 
често се окислява и в тази връзка повърхността е посребрена. Дъгогъсенето на контакторите е с 
електромагнитно издължаване на дъгата. 
Комисията е констатирала силно разтопени контакти от превключвателя на степени и

### 5. Conclusions 
### 5.1. Summarized analysis of the causes of the occurrence 
The investigating commission carried out several inspections of the fire-damaged locomotive No. 
91520044144-1 at the Plovdiv Locomotive Depot, and reviewed the provided documentation regarding 
the technical condition (operation and repairs) of the locomotive prior to the accident. It conducted 
interviews with the operations and repair managers at the Plovdiv Locomotive Depot. 
The investigating commission analysed the findings and the information gathered after the 
accident, as well as the documents provided and the results of the interviews with the staff involved, and 
analysed the causal, contributing and systemic factors set out in Regulation (EU) 2020/572, according to 
their definitions. 
Taking into account the results and measurements carried out on the locomotive after the accident, 
the commission concluded that the occurrence was caused by the poor technical condition of locomotive 
No. 91520044144-1, resulting from improper maintenance and repair. 
On the transition from traction mode to coasting, contact S3 remained in a half-open position owing 
to a worn cam, which prevented the breaking of the electric arc and led to a rise in temperature and the 
ignition of the contact in the ATC. 
### 5.2. Measures taken following the occurrence 
The infrastructure manager took timely action to ensure the restoration of the timetable and of the 
capacity of the railway infrastructure on Main Railway Line No. 9. Train movements on the open line 
were restored at 19:25h on 17.04.2026, on schedule. 
### 5.3. Additional findings 
In the course of the investigation into the fire onboard locomotive No. 91520044144-1, the 
investigating commission established that (within a period of six months) the S-contacts of ATC had failed 
on five other locomotives at the same Plovdiv Locomotive Depot. 
Chronology of the identified cases: 
• On 02.09.2025, locomotive No. 91520044065-8 was received for scheduled maintenance (to the 
extent of a technical examination), accompanied by the removal and subsequent repair of the 4th wheelset. 
The locomotive was operating with ATC No. 45818, without any registered issues until then. The last 
oscillogram taken was dated 13.03.2025. 
• On 10.09.2025, locomotive No. 91520044061-7 was received for scheduled maintenance (to the 
extent of a technical examination), without any registered prior issues in the operation of ATC No. 45598. 
The oscillogram taken confirmed its serviceability as of 07.08.2025. 
The repairs on both locomotives were completed on 11.09.2025; on both ATC unit. Four worn S-
contacts on each were replaced with new ones supplied by “Trade Engineering” EOOD. During the 
functional tests and acceptance of the locomotives by the operations commission, melted power resistors 
were found on the contactor blocks of both autotransformer tap-changer units. The checks established 
stuck S-contacts. The damaged ATC units were removed. A spare ATC was fitted on locomotive No. 
91520044061-7, and the locomotive was returned to service on 16.09.2025. A spare ATC No. 45924 was 
fitted on locomotive No. 91520044065-8, and the locomotive was returned to service on 25.09.2025. 
• On 10.03.2026, locomotive No. 91520044155-7 entered for scheduled maintenance (to the extent 
of a technical examination), with no identified previous issues in the operation of ATC No. 45604. The 
last oscillogram check was during the annual planned repair in November 2025. During servicing, two 
worn contacts of the ATC were replaced with newly supplied ones from “Trade Engineering” EOOD. 
During subsequent functional tests, the resistors of the contactor block of the ATC melted as a result of 
the welding of the 1st and 2nd groups of contacts. The ATC was removed, overhauled and subsequently 
refitted to the same locomotive.  
• On 01.04.2026, locomotive No. 91520044065-8 entered for repair on demand (the fault was

4. The chemical composition of the publicly procured contactor is similar to that of the original, but

### 6. Safety recommendations 
Having taken into account the causal, contributing and systemic factors identified during the 
investigation, and with a view to preventing other similar occurrences in the future, the investigating 
commission issues safety recommendations in accordance with the provisions of Article 94(1) of 
Ordinance No. 59 on safety management in railway transport. These recommendations were addressed to 
the Railway Administration Executive Agency (RAEA — the National Safety Authority). The latter 
should take the necessary measures within its remit and powers to ensure that the safety recommendations 
issued by the NTIB are taken into account and passed on to the railway undertaking and to the 
infrastructure manager. 
• Recommendation 1: the State Enterprise National Railway Infrastructure Company (NRIC) and 
“BDZ – Passenger Services” EOOD should familiarise the relevant staff with the content of this report; 
• Recommendation 2: In the event of a breakdown of a pair of contacts on the contactor block of 
the ATCs, BDZ Passenger Services EOOD should replace all contact bodies of the unit; 
• Recommendation 3: BDZ Passenger Services EOOD should design and deploy equipment and 
technology for the diagnostics of autotransformer tap-changers, providing information on the technical 
condition of the unit; 
• Recommendation 4: BDZ Passenger Services EOOD should perform technical diagnostics of 
the ATCs during each technical examination to ensure periodic monitoring of the unit and, where 
necessary, undertake repair works; 
### • Recommendation 5: BDZ Passenger Services EOOD should develop a methodology for 
objective assessment of the condition of ATCs based on objectively recorded parameters and 
measurements; 
In line with the requirements of Article 24(2) of Directive (EU) 2016/798 of the European 
Parliament and of the Council, and Article 91(3) of Ordinance No. 59 from 5.12.2006, the member of the 
Management Board of the NTIB submitted on 04.09.2026 a final report containing information on the 
investigated occurrence together with the issued safety recommendations, with a view to improving safety 
in the railway transport of the Republic of Bulgaria.
