1 Summary 
The first section contains a brief description of the event, as well as information on the 
consequences, primary causes and safety recommendations provided in the individual case. 
1.1 Brief description of the event 
At around 07:29 on 26 August 2025, passenger train DPN 83205 collided with a passenger car, 
on the journey from Bremerhaven-Lehe Pbf to Twistringen at the level crossing (BÜ) in km 
131.702 between the operating points Bf Bremen-Burg and Hp Bremen-Oslebshausen. 
1.2 Consequences 
Property damage amounting to an estimated EUR 1.3 million was caused to the railway 
vehicle, infrastructure and passenger car involved. The road user was fatally injured. Two 
other people were slightly injured.

# 5 Conclusions 
The following section contains a summary of the identified causal, contributing and systemic 
factors. In addition, two further subsections are provided containing information about 
measures already taken, and additional comments 
### 5.1 Summary and conclusion 
In the context of the event investigation, the BEU identified two causal, one contributing and 
one systemic factors. 
Contributing factor: Design planning assuming a different maximum line speed [F1] 
The fact that the design planning for the NWS was based on a maximum line speed different 
from the VzG was considered by the BEU as a contributing factor. The faulty design had had 
an impact on the trackside position of the switch-on contacts for the NWS in the specific event.  
Taking into account the actual maximum permissible line speed of 140 km/h in the design, 
instead of the assumed 160 km/h, would have led to a change in the trackside position of the 
NWS switch-on contacts by approximately 110 m, closer to the BÜ. The error in the design 
planning was not effectively detected by the underlying verification processes in the EIU SMS.  
On the systemic factor: Plausibility check of the draft planning for the NWS [S1] 
Although the EIU generally held processes in the SMS with regard to the plausibility check of 
a draft planning, the actual implementation and suitability of a planned plausibility check 
could not be demonstrated in the present case. Contrary to Delegated Regulation (EU) 
2018/762, Annex II, Chapter 4.4.3, relevant decision-making processes could therefore not be 
transparently traced in terms of correctness, consistency and control of safety information. 
However, according to the BEU, these above-mentioned processes are the essential 
prerequisite to ensure that the use of TH BÜP with mobSi and NWS, as provided for by the 
EIU, only minimises risks in operation. Even if the EIU is already striving to further develop the 
NWS with regard to suitable technical possibilities, these upstream process steps of design 
planning for operational use will continue to be essential in the future. Therefore, the 
consistency and correctness of safety information for the downstream design of the property, 
plant and equipment must also be ensured and reproducible. The BEU therefore recommends 
safety recommendation No 04/2026 on the systemic factor [S1]. It is recommended to 
improve the underlying planning, approval and documentation processes for the design

### The causal factor: Opening the barriers on approaching train [F2] 
Securing a BÜ by BÜP represents an increased risk due to the fact that an individual’s error 
can directly lead to an accident. The untimely opening of the mobSi by the BÜP despite the 
approaching train journey was a violation of the regulations of Ril 456. This action created a 
situation with a high potential for danger, as train journey DPN 83205 approached the BÜ at 
that time. The BÜP was highly likely to have forgotten the train journey, despite the previous 
written entry in the ‘proof of notifications’. When he became aware of this, he immediately 
initiated the re-closing of the barrier system, which, however, could no longer prevent the 
collision between the rail vehicle and the car. The TH-BÜP property, plant and equipment with 
NWS brought in by the EIU was intended to reduce the occurrence of hazards, but did not 
meet this requirement in the present event, see the above-mentioned explanatory notes to 
[F1] and [S1]. 
In the past, following the serious railway crossing accident between the Neustadt am 
Rübenberge (a. Rbge.) and Hagen (Han) stations, the Federal Railway Accident Investigation 
Office issued several safety recommendations relating to reliable item security on 15 May 
2017. As a result of the safety recommendations, the EIU further developed, among other 
things, the NWS to minimise risks caused by mishandling a BÜP on the BÜ. The BEU considers 
the continued further development and use of NWS on alternatively secured by BÜPs in 
principle positive, since the NWS in combination with a TH-BÜP generated an increase in 
security compared to the previously solely dependent on human action, for example by means 
of barrier bands. Due to the NWS and technically based emergency closure, railway crossing 
accidents throughout Germany could be avoided in the past. 
On the day of the event, a system from the manufacturer UPZ Sitech GmbH was used on the 
‘Grambker Dorfstraße’ BÜ, which detected the approach of a train journey to the BÜ on a 
time-based basis by means of switch-on contacts on the trackside. In contrast to a regular 
technical BÜ fuse, the NWS used did not currently have track contacts that affect the 
elimination of the opening prevention time of the NWS. For the system used on the day of the 
event, the opening prevention time and the local position of the switch-on contacts had been 
determined by planning using the wrong maximum permissible line speed.

The BEU recommends that, in order to control the risks arising from the operation of the 
installation in accordance with the requirements of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/762, 
Annex II, Chapter 3.1.1., when replacing a BÜ with technical solutions, these should be 
reviewed and further developed with regard to the switch-off criteria and barrier opening. For 
this reason, the BEU recommends safety recommendation 05/2026 on the causal factor [F2]. 
The causal factor: Road users enter the BÜ when light signals are switched on [F3] 
Driving on a BÜ when the light sign system is switched on represents a situation with danger 
to life and limb. In the case of the present event at the ‘Grambker Dorfstraße’ BÜ, there was 
an infringement of Paragraph 19(2)(2) of the StVO, since the road user did not wait for the 
extinguishing of the light sign, which still showed red light, when he perceived the opening 
barriers, and did not drive the BÜ in time. In accordance with the objective of the BEU set out 
in Chapter 2, no questions of guilt or liability are relevant in the context of this accident 
investigation. These were not the subject of the investigation and are not the subject of the 
presentation in the investigation report.  
### 5.2 Measures taken since the event 
The BÜP was blocked for further use by the EIU after the event. 
After the event, the replacement fuse on the ‘Grambker Dorfstraße’ BÜ was managed in 
accordance with the instructions ‘No 815.8010-W-102-!’ (version 2.0) and the full barrier 
closure of the mobSi was rebuilt on a semi-barrier system. The planning speed, which was 
incorrectly chosen too high in the project planning, was reduced to the correct value of 
140 km/h in accordance with the VzG and the project planning was revised accordingly. As a 
result, the track-side position of the switch-on contacts for the NWS was also adjusted. 
At the time of the report, the regular technical security of the BÜ ‘Grambker Dorfstraße’ was 
restored after the completion of the construction project in accordance with Betra. 
Furthermore, the EIU informed that an implementation plan for the further development of 
the NWS was drawn up by the specialist departments at the headquarters of DB InfraGO AG, 
which was submitted to the BEU. In the broadest sense, the realization of a draught-induced 
elimination of the NWS is planned – in contrast to the current, temporary effect of the NWS 
by means of opening prevention time.

According to the EIU, the aim is to harmonise with the manufacturers on the basis of currently 
differing product solutions.  
Several scenarios relating to the BÜP had been drawn up. These would be in coordination 
between the EIU and the manufacturers with regard to further measures: 
a) Procedure BÜP secures BÜ in time: The train must use the first axle to enter the switch-
off point in the direction of travel behind the BÜ. 
b) Procedure BÜP does not secure BÜ in time: It is currently being worked out. 
c) Procedure BÜP early cancellation of the BÜ fuse: It is currently being worked out. 
d) Procedure for multi-track BÜ: It is currently being worked out. 
The aim of the EIU is to forego the approach time in the future. 
After that, the requirements will be merged and the existing specifications will be adapted. 
The specifications are then to be finalised with the manufacturers for the respective NWS.
