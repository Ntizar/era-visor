1 Summary 
The first section contains a brief description of the event, as well as information on the 
consequences, primary causes and safety recommendations provided in the individual case. 
1.1 Brief description of the event 
On 24.09.2024 about 07:15 a.m. the passenger train DPN-G 75332 collided on the journey 
from Duisburg Hauptbahnhof (Hbf) to Xanten in the station Rheinkamp on the switch 41 with 
a not point-marker-free stationary locomotive double-unit. 
1.2 Consequences 
Four people were seriously injured and twelve were slightly injured. Damage to the railway 
vehicles involved amounted to approximately €834,500.

#  5 Conclusions 
The following section contains a summary of the identified causal, contributing and systemic 
factors. In addition, two further subsections are provided containing information about 
measures already taken, and additional comments 
### 5.1 Summary and conclusion 
The acts, omissions, incidents or circumstances identified in this investigation report around 
the event period led to the train collision at Rheinkamp station. Two causal factors affecting 
the event were identified by the BEU. 
The causal factor ‘Failure to check the point marker-free position of the double-unit’[F1]: 
The event was caused by a work error on the part of the Tf, which did not set the double unit 
out of switch 41’s point marker. This probably happened under great time pressure, as the Tf 
did not want to delay the continuation of the DPN-G 75332, which had been waiting for 20 
minutes. In principle, it should be noted that the control of the consequences of human error 
in the interconnection of RUs and IMs must be a joint task of railway compagnies. In addition 
to the RU-side training and raising Tf's awareness of the point-marker-free parking of vehicles, 
it may be necessary to examine the extent to which organisational and technical measures on 
the part of the EIU are also suitable for controlling the effects of human error.  
### The causal factor ‘failure of the signaller to check the track clearance’ [F2]: 
In the Rheinkamp district, it was found that the checks for clearance of vehicles in the area of 
the event were carried out by watching and thus exclusively under human safety 
responsibility, as technical safety equipment was not available. Errors in the course of 
clearance-checking usually have direct effects and can inevitably lead to hazards or accidents. 
In the present case, the competent Fdl had not correctly evaluated the track sections in its 
district. This short rule oversight sufficed in conjunction with the usual operating procedures 
and led to the train collision on the switch 41. With a proper check from the window, the Fdl 
would have been able to see beyond doubt that there was a vehicle parked in the roadway 
that was not marker-free. Higher automation of interlocking systems would not have allowed 
this human error in this form. The Federal Railway Accident Investigation Office had 
recommended safety recommendation No. 01/2018

‘Train stations that do not currently have automatic track clearance systems should

This safety recommendation is unchanged with regard to what happened in Rheinkamp at the

### 5.2 Measures taken since the event 
Following the event in Rheinkamp, DB InfraGO AG conducted an employee interview with the 
Fdl. In the future, this should no longer be used in the function as Fdl. 
The EVU Rail Cargo Carrier - Germany GmbH blocked the Tf due to the event until the 
reconstruction of the event and carried out a follow-up training. 
The topics identified during the event were included in the regular training courses. A 
presentation and evaluation of the event was carried out in the so-called ‘Safety Newsletter’. 
In the monitoring discussions with Tf, the necessary operating procedures for forming and 
solving double-tractions are dealt with within the expert discussions conducted in this context. 
Furthermore, the RU issued an operational instruction on ‘Registration of shunting operations 
in the digital GSM-R train radio without shunting in shunting radio groups (RiR) procedure’ in 
addition to the Tf rule book valid for the RU. In this case, all shunting movements that take 
place before or after train journeys are to be carried out exclusively by indicating the train 
number when dialing into the GSM-R in order to ensure the recording of the conversations. 
In the second half of 2026, an electronic interlocking for the Moers-Xanten line will be put into 
operation. As a result, the source of errors described at the time of the report is no longer 
available in the foreseeable future.

# 6 Safety recommendations 
No safety recommendations were made. Since there is a well-established and tried-and-tested 
risk control measure for the timely and inconsequential detection of such a work error in 
accordance with the rules governing the parking of vehicles, no independent safety 
recommendation is issued for the causal factor [F1]. 
Safety Recommendation No 01/2018 remains relevant with regard to the events in Rheinkamp 
at the time of the event. Through the project ‘Electronic interlocking Lower Rhine 3rd and 4th 
Construction stage’ on the line between Rheinberg (Rhl) and Xanten and at Rheinkamp station, 
the train control and safety technology will be replaced by modern electronic interlocking 
technology by the end of 2026, so that the risk of errors in the course of a roadway test by 
looking at the Rheinkamp station will be excluded in the future. For these reasons, no 
independent safety recommendation is made for the causal factor [F2].
