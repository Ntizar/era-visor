Summary 
On the 9th February 2017 at 22.50 the section “B” of diesel locomotive 
2TE10M No.3424 (hereinafter- diesel locomotive) caught on fire on the route No.10 at 
Jelgava station.   
Diesel engine of section “B” of diesel locomotive was put in motion with the 
help by moving high pressure fuel pump barell to the fuel supply increase direction. 
After a short while diesel engine started to operate in abnormal regime. All the efforts 
of locomotive brigade to disrupt the operation of diesel engine failed. Diesel engine 
room heavily filled with smoke and fire started at turbo compressor area. Locomotive 
brigade was trying to extinguish the fire, by using stationary fire fighting foam of 
section “A” of diesel, but due to the smoke and the threat to the health, it failed. 
The cause of fire of diesel locomotive was the operation of engine of section 
“B” of this diesel locomotive in abnormal regime.  
Meanwhile the operation of diesel engine in abnormal regime (overspeed) was 
caused by turning around the axis of high pressure fuel pump barell, as a result of it 
leads of barell lost connection with high pressure fuel pump toothed rack, which were 
at maximum fuel supply condition.   
During the investigation three safety recommendations were elaborated, which 
contains the following areas:  
• Inventory for the shift of components of diesel engine of the diesel 
locomotive; 
• Control of maintenance and repair of diesel locomotive; 
• Amendments to the legislation in relation with the investigation of fire of 
rolling stock. 
Safety recommendations 
Recommendation 2018-1  
LDZ Rolling Stock Service, Ltd, to implement procedures in order for the shifted 
component for every diesel locomotive to be listed during the technical maintenance 
and  renovation repair, including barell and bracket detail, in order to control the 
amount of repair and the duration of component exploitation.  
Recommendation 2018-2 
LDZ Rolling Stock Service, Ltd, to implement procedures, in order the performance 
of technical maintenance and renovation repair of diesel locomotive to be examined 
and examination to be documented in order to control the scope and quality of 
performed works. 
Recommendation 2018-3 
State Railway technical inspectorate to provide proposals to the amendments to 
legal enactments, in order to provide in the future in the legislation the norm to 
investigate the fire of rolling stock, which are not serious accidents.
