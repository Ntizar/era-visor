Summary  
On 26 July 2005, at 11:28 clock, at the station of Gramatneusiedl, in the area of points 6 and 7, there 
occured a train collision (flank collision) between freight train Z 75173, which entered station track 5 
coming from Himberg from line track 2, and regional train Z 2618, which passed the stopsignal in 
station track 1 in direction of Himberg.   
After the scheduled stop Z 2618, was dispatched despite there was no given approval (clear signal), 
continued the trip and accelerated in assuming to meet the departure signal “clear”. During the 
approach to the signal the approaching train Z 75173 was noticed, but the “stop” indicating signal 
wasn’t recognized. This signal was recognized very short time before reaching the location of the 
signal, an emergency break was initiated. 
In the collision, 4 passengers seriously injured, 9 passengers and 3 employees of the railway 
company slightly injured. The vehicles, the infrastructure and the technical equipment was damaged 
in parts serious. Train operation was interrupted for several days. International travel and freight 
trains have been diverted spacious, for regional trains and commuter trains rail replacement services 
was implemented.
