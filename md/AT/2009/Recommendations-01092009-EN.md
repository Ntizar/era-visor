A-68/2011 
Investigate whether the alert limit, intervention limit and 
manager 
11.5 
immediate action limit for cant excess and deficiency should be 
redefined in staff instruction DB IS 2 – Part 1 (source [1]):  
Section and track category:  
In section category S and 1 in track category a in all other 
section categories and tracks  
Justification – source [1]: an error of ± 26 mm in the super-elevation 
(cant) produces an increase [in lateral acceleration] of 0.17 m/s² in 
curves. That means that a vehicle running through curves with a 
nominal lateral acceleration of 0.85 m/s² has an actual uncompensated 
lateral acceleration of 1.02 m/s². Hence, if the permissible 
uncompensated lateral acceleration is used to calculate the permitted 
speed of a section of line then for an acceleration of  
 
0.65 m/s² lateral acceleration will in fact be more than 25% 
higher and for  
 
0.85 m/s² more than 20 % higher.  
A new vehicle being evaluated for approval however, need only comply 
with a 10 % excess over the limiting values for safety in movement. 
With good reason therefore, the cant in [ÖBB] staff regulation DV B52 
[Technical Principles for Permanent Way] is limited to ± 10 mm in the 
unloaded condition. Accordingly the consequences for the vehicle 
halve, as is not difficult to calculate. At the time of the derailment, [ÖBB] 
staff regulation DV B52 however had not been completely withdrawn. It 
may therefore be assumed that in practice the interaction of vehicles or 
the properties of safety in movement are not assessed correctly 
because the immediate action limit is missing.

A-69/2011 
Investigate whether the technical preconditions - to be able to 
manager 
11.6 
determine the difference between the design cant and the actual 
cant measured - should be satisfied on permanent way 
measurement vehicle EM250.  
Justification: the cant excess or deficiency is defined as the difference 
between the design cant and the actual cant. The data bases with the 
lists of curves with information on design cant are not currently directly 
available on the permanent way measurement vehicle and are 
therefore not shown on the measurement log. In addition, they are not 
added directly to the data and compared with it. It is not currently 
possible to identify non-compliance directly by means of an EM250 
permanent way log. Hence it is only possible to discover that an 
immediate action limit or an intervention limit has been exceeded in the 
post-processing procedure. In curves, in particular, not taking account 
of the fact that actual cant exceeds the limits of deviation from the 
design cant can lead to unbalanced lateral acceleration exceeding 
permitted limits.
