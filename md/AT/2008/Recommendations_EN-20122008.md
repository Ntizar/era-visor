Extract from the investigation report BMVIT-795.122-

17. Recommendations 
Point 
Safety recommendation 
Addressed to 
17.1 
Check if at all Austrian HOA/FOA/SOA the distance of the defined 
IM 
stopping point from the installation has been kept as short as possible. 
Reason: Had train 44852 stopped at a previously (yet to be defined) designated 
point, the consequences of the accident could, most probably, have been 
reduced. 
17.2 
Check the process instructions to see if the envisaged stopping points for 
IM 
the investigation of the causes in the light of point 17.1 could be brought 
forward by the traction unit driver. 
Reason: Had train 44852 stopped at a previously (yet to be defined) designated 
point the consequences of the accident could, most probably, have been 
reduced. 
17.3 
Check if electronic signalling to notify the traction unit driver of hot boxes 
IM  
is possible. Here it is necessary to check how this information can be 
transmitted with ETCS. 
Reason: Had train 44852 stopped at a previously (yet to be defined) designated 
point, the consequences of the accident could, most probably, have been 
reduced. 
17.4 
Include Annex 1 of the process instructions HOA/FOA/SOA, ‘List of 
IM 
defined absolute stop signals, train handling yards, wagon investigation 
yards, HOA/FOA/SOA in rear, train observation points and safety 
measures for the traction unit driver on multiple-track lines on the open 
track’ in the Bsb of the Hallwang – Elixhausen yard. 
Reason: These failings have already been acknowledged by ÖBB Infrastruktur AG 
and remedied. 
17.5 
Record and clear defects on all vehicles derailed during shunting in order 
RU 
to avoid or be able to understand subsequent wheelset damage. 
Reason: Wheelset damage that is not dealt with can lead to serious accidents. 
RU 
17.6 
In order to avoid a repeat of such an event in the future, the following 
Vehicle 
measures have been implemented by Technical Services: 
maintenance 
• increase the dimensional overlap of components with a press fit 
provider 
between the bearing inner race and axle stub when reconditioning 
wheelsets in the Knittelfeld works; 
• increase the quality of the bearing grease used; 
• check if meaningful investigations of the grease quality prior to a 
wheelset refurbishment are possible for IS 3. 
Reason: Improvements in production processes. 
Rail safety 
17.7

authority 
approval by the authorities.
